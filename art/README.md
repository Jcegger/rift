## Print files

- `print/token-keep-lid-87x113mm-2mm-bleed.png` — a lid skin for a Gamegenic Token Keep
  (outside 113 × 87 mm, per gamegenic.com), standing upright. 2150 × 2764 px at 600 dpi:
  87 × 113 mm trim plus 2 mm bleed on every side. The whole piece, scaled to 79 × 109 mm, sits
  centred on a near-black frame, so nothing is cropped: not the ribbon, not the tray, not the
  artist's stamp. **Measure the lid's flat top before ordering**: 113 × 87 is the box's
  outside size, and a raised rim, logo or rounded corners would make the printable area smaller.

## Accessory box — in progress (Jay, from 16 Nov 2026)

The plan: a **Gamegenic Token Keep** (113 × 87 × 36 mm outside, 94 × 68 × 28 mm inside, per
gamegenic.com; about $10–13) for dice, tokens and counters, with the art as a **matte laminated
vinyl sticker** on the lid. Sleeves are done: Ryan ordered them.

1. Buy the Token Keep (black matches the frame).
2. **Measure the lid's flat top.** If it is smaller than 87 × 113 mm, regenerate the print file
   at that size: same layout, whole piece on the near-black frame, 2 mm bleed, 600 dpi.
3. Order the sticker: rectangle, rounded corners (2–3 mm), matte laminate, 3.43 × 4.45 in
   (87 × 113 mm) or the measured size; tell the printer the file includes bleed.
   - Sticker Mule (stickermule.com/custom-stickers): matte, any size, free proof, ~4-day
     turnaround, **10 minimum** (the spares are useful).
   - No minimum: StickerYou, customstickers.com. Standout Stickers: matte included, minimum
     not checked. Check prices and minimums when ordering.
4. Fill it: a 45 mm score dial, a few d6s, Might (+2, +5), buff and Gold tokens. A Riftbound
   set: BuyTheSameToken's deluxe set (56 tokens, dial, organiser; third-party). A pen and a mini
   pad for written score at higher-level events (TR §415.3) will not fit, so carry them separately.
