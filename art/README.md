## Cafe Cuties Sivir (November 2026)

Commissioned piece for the Sivir deck (`guides/sivir-build-sheet.md`): Sivir
in a Cafe Cuties cafe outfit, holding a coffee cup and her bladed chakram.

- Artist: Ryan Wu
- Delivered November 16, 2026, by email ("Cafe Cuties Sivir Commission
  Assets").
- Delivered as:
  - `cafesivirfin2.png` — finished art, 4352 x 6000 PNG (email attachment).
  - `cafesivirfin.clip` — Clip Studio Paint source file (Google Drive link in
    the delivery email).

The `.clip` source is kept locally and gitignored (`art/*.clip`): it is 31 MB, and
this repo is published at rift.jayegger.com.

## Print files

- `print/token-keep-lid-87x113mm-2mm-bleed.png` — a lid skin for a Gamegenic Token Keep
  (outside 113 × 87 mm, per gamegenic.com), standing upright. 2150 × 2764 px at 600 dpi:
  87 × 113 mm trim plus 2 mm bleed on every side. The whole piece, scaled to 79 × 109 mm, sits
  centred on a near-black frame, so nothing is cropped: not the ribbon, not the tray, not the
  artist's stamp. **Measure the lid's flat top before ordering**: 113 × 87 is the box's
  outside size, and a raised rim, logo or rounded corners would make the printable area smaller.
