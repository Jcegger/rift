the legends with real results and no Astral Heron (Jay's exclusion), Kai'Sa and Kennen were
the two with results; Kennen shares much of Sivir's Chaos toolkit (Fizz 85%, Star-Crossed 84%,

## 1. Identity — the reads (24 Oct 2026)

**The legend.** *Daughter of the Void* (Fury/Mind): *"[E]: [Reaction] — Add [A]. Use only to
play spells."* An extra Power of any colour each turn, at Reaction speed, for spells only.
Where Sivir's legend paid for hiding, this one pays for removal on either player's turn.

**What the experts say she is** (riftbound.gg's Radiance guide, Den, 22–23 Oct; the Day 1
meta report, 22 Oct):
- **Tempo, not control.** "Removal spells help her units conquer without having to brute force
  their way in"; her units are low Might for their cost with strong abilities, and draw keeps
  them coming. If the early game goes her way she conquers every turn; if the opponent stops
  her small units scoring, she slows down and plays for value with removal. (The repo's own
  measure calls the crowd list "control / spells"; the guide wins, per CLAUDE.md's order.)
- **The Riven counter.** Removing units at battlefields denies Riven's "enter ready". Calm
  counters (Defy) get in the way.
- **Kennen is a bad matchup.** Reactive decks struggle when several Nocturnes land at once,
  and Kennen almost never runs out of cards.

**Her champion units** (all legal):

| card | cost | text |
|---|---|---|
| **Kai'Sa, Survivor** | 4E, 4 Might, Fury | Accelerate; when I conquer, draw 1 — **the champion in all 13 placed lists** |
| Kai'Sa, Rebel | 5E 1P, 5 Might, Mind | Disarm; while I'm in a showdown, your spells have Reaction |
| Kai'Sa, Evolutionary | 6E 1P, 6 Might, Mind | Ganking; when I conquer, play a spell from your trash costing less than your points, free, then recycle it |

Her signature is **Icathian Rain** (7E 3P): six instructions of "deal 2 to a unit".

**The results.** 57 legal lists since 16 October, 13 with a placing; best: 2nd of 74 (the
Nova Showdown final), 12th and 14th of 74. Grades: A (median of 4 creators, "agree").

**The placed lists agree on a core.** In all 13 placed lists: Kai'Sa, Survivor; Hextech Ray;
Falling Star ×3; Stupefy ×3; Smoke Screen; Bellows Breath; Bilgewater Tracker; Noxus Hopeful;
Watchful Sentry ×3; Plundering Poro ×3; Brynhir Thundersong ×3; Thousand-Tailed Watcher ×3;
Temporal Breach ×3; Time Warp; runes 7 Fury / 5 Mind; battlefields Void Gate, Rockfall Path,
Frozen Fortress. Then Lecturing Yordle (85%), The Empyrean (77%), Lost to the Sands (62%),
Ferrous Forerunner (46%).

**How it fares against the field** (its crowd list, the same measures as Sivir; not a win
rate): Defy would counter about 5.9 of its spells (Sivir 3.6); 1.4 answer copies per regular
6+ Might unit, none free (Sivir 10.0, 3 free); exposure to Might-limited removal 22% (Sivir
29%); 3.0 units by turn 3 (Sivir 3.1). The low big-unit count reads its removal unit by unit;
its own answer to big units is damage stacking and Watcher's −3, which the measure doesn't see.

## The choices for stage 1 (Jay's)

1. **The feel.** The guide's tempo plan (remove, conquer, draw), or lean slower into a value
   control shape? This sets everything after it.
2. **How far from the core.** The 13 placed lists agree on about 20 cards. Start from that core
   and argue every slot (as Sivir started from Randyyy's list), or build up from the plan with
   the core only as evidence?
3. **The champion.** Survivor (every placed list), or a case for Rebel (spells at Reaction in
   fights) or Evolutionary (free spells from the trash)?
4. **The goal.** Nexus Night like Sivir (best of three, 50-minute rounds, casual field), or
   something more competitive (a Skirmish)?
5. **Anything off the table** beyond Astral Heron, and as always Dazzling Aurora.
