How this works: one opponent per section, built from its **Radiance one-on-one tournament results**
(falling back to good finishes, then posted lists, and saying so), the full text of its cards, and
the rules where an interaction decides something. For each: how it wins, what it fields against
us, how **v1** (*Sivir Is Trying Her Best*) and **v2** (*Sivir v2 (draft)*, `sivir-v2.md`) play the
early, middle and late game, the interactions that decide it, and how sure the reading is.
**No win rates**: nothing here has been played. The point is which version is better placed,
and why, so the journal knows what to look for.

**The two versions, for reference.** Shared: Traveling Merchant 3, Evelynn 1, Staunch 2, Reckless
Innovator 3, Fizz 3, Ezreal 3, Tail-Cloaked Matriarch 3, Vex 2, Rengar, Trophy Hunter 2, Sivir;
Punch First 3, Sabotage 2, Star-Crossed 2, Switcheroo 2, Rebuke 1; Edge of Night 2. **v1 only:**
Tornado Warrior 2, Rampage 3. **v2 only:** Graves, Blasting Through 2, Beast Below 1, Rampage 2.

## Master Yi, Wuju Bladesman (S) — Control · Holds

**Evidence:** 5 Radiance results, all LDXP Sacramento 25k, **mid-pack** (#42, #113, #114, #139,
#147 of 755). The thinnest S-tier evidence; the field forecast's S is mostly the experts' grade.

**What he plays** (share of the 5 lists): the legend gives **+2 Might to a unit defending alone**.
- *Bodies, mostly small:* Pit Rookie 3 (2 Might, buffs another), First Mate ×2 (3, readies
  another), Lonely Poro ×2 (2, draws if it dies alone), Scuttle Crab ×3 (0, draws; on death
  reveals your hand and **your face-down cards**), Master Yi, Tempered (4; Level 6: Deflect, Ganking).
- *Big:* **Rengar, Trophy Hunter ×3** (6, Ambush) and **Ruin Runner ×2** (6 Energy, 5 Might,
  **can't be chosen** by your spells and abilities).
- *Tricks and answers, 100% of lists:* Defy ×3, Discipline ×3 (+2, draw, Reaction), Punch First ×3,
  Charm ×3 (move an enemy unit; **no Action or Reaction, so main phase only**), Zhonya's ×1.6,
  Rampage ×2, Onslaught (+6), and Sabotage ×2 (80%), En Garde, Not So Fast (60%).

**How he wins:** a single defender on each battlefield he holds, made bigger by the legend and by
cheap Reactions the moment you commit. Charm moves your units off battlefields on his turn; Rengar
and Ruin Runner are the threats that take yours. It is the same Body toolbox as yours (Punch First,
Rampage, Sabotage, Rengar) plus the Calm protection you don't have (Defy, Discipline, Zhonya's).

**The interactions that decide it**
1. **His +2 only exists while a unit defends alone.** Attack with two, or remove the defender
   outside combat: **Rampage** (no fight, so no +2), **Rebuke**, **Evelynn's pull** (she moves his
   unit to *your* battlefield, where it isn't defending), and in v2 **Beast Below**.
2. **Ruin Runner can't be chosen**, so Rebuke, Star-Crossed, Rampage, Switcheroo and Beast Below all
   miss it. What reaches it: a fight (Rengar or Mercenary at 6, Punch First +5), **Vex's stun when it is
   played** (it doesn't choose), and in v2 **Graves's blast** (it doesn't choose either: 4, or 9 with
   Punch First, against its 5 Might).
3. **Defy is in every list**, and it reaches your Rampage, Star-Crossed and Sabotage. Your Punch First,
   Switcheroo and Rebuke are out of its reach, and so is any unit, Beast Below included (Not So Fast,
   60%, can counter Beast's bounce, since it chooses his unit).
4. **His Sabotage strips your tricks from hand.** Face-down cards are out of its reach, so hide the
   Switcheroo and Edge you mean to keep. His Scuttle Crab sees your face-down cards when it dies.
5. **He has no bounce.** Nothing in his lists returns a unit to hand, so a Vex that lands at a
   battlefield sticks unless Charm moves it or a fight kills it.

**v1, played through**
- *Early:* both sides put cheap bodies on battlefields. His lone defenders plus Discipline make
  your 2- and 3-Might attackers lose fights; your attacks need two units or a Power-paid pump he
  can't Defy (Punch First).
- *Middle:* Vex is good here (no bounce to remove her; his new units arrive stunned). Rampage is
  poor: Defy, Discipline in response, and Zhonya's all blunt it.
- *Late:* Ruin Runner is the problem: two copies, unchoosable, and v1's only ways through are a
  fight or catching it with Vex as it lands. His Rengars meet yours at equal size.

**v2, played through**
- *Early:* the same, with one difference: two fewer 3-drops (no Tornado), so turn 3's double play is
  a little rarer.
- *Middle:* **Graves defending your hold** blasts his attackers for 4 before damage (attackers don't
  get his +2), which kills Pit Rookie, First Mate, Lonely Poro and Crab outright. **Beast Below**
  bounces his lone defender off a battlefield without a fight and replays your Ezreal.
- *Late:* **Graves with Punch First blasts for 9**, the one card here that kills Ruin Runner without
  choosing it. One Rampage fewer costs little: it was your weakest card in this matchup.
- *Risks:* his Charm can move Graves off the battlefield he defends (main phase, so on his turn:
  Repulse in the sideboard counters it); a lone defender at 4+2 survives a plain 4-damage blast.

**Reading: v2 is better placed, modestly.** It adds the matchup's two missing tools: a way through
Ruin Runner that doesn't choose (Graves with Punch First), and a way to clear a lone defender
without a fight (Beast Below). It gives up the card that was weakest here (a Rampage) and two
bodies that didn't matter much. **How sure:** medium-low. The rules interactions are checked, but his
lists are five mid-pack finishes from one event.

**For the journal:** Ruin Runner turns (did anything get through it?), how often Charm broke your
hold, and whether Vex stuck.

## Riven, The Exile (S) — Midrange · Holds

**Evidence:** 46 Radiance results from 2 events (the 25k; Club9 Clash #3): the most of any legend.
Best #14 of 755. The detailed plan for v1 is in the guide ("Against Riven, in detail").

**What she plays:** the legend makes units she plays to battlefields **she controls** enter ready.
- *Bodies:* Bedeviling Stranger ×2.4 (2, Hidden; bounces one of yours if 4+ units are there), Tideturner
  ×2.4 (2, Hidden), Scuttle Crab ×2.3 (0), Reckless Innovator ×2.8 (3), Stellacorn Herder ×2.9 (3, draws
  as it moves), Riven, Broken Blade (4; on exhausting: buff, stun, or ready), Blitzcrank ×1.5 (5;
  pulls one of your units to its battlefield).
- *Top end:* **Astral Heron ×3** (7; at a battlefield, her next card costs 2 Energy and 2 Power less)
  and **Baron Nashor ×1.3** (12, can't be chosen, her other units +2, the Baron Pit).
- *Equipment and protection:* **Blade of the Exile ×3 in every list** (+3; equips by exhausting a unit,
  which triggers Broken Blade and Innovator), Zhonya's ×2.9 (93%).
- *Answers:* Defy ×2.8 and Discipline ×3 (100%), Star-Crossed 89%, Rebuke 87%, Switcheroo 83%, Gust 72%,
  Not So Fast 54%, **her own Vex, Apathetic 37%**, Ki Burst 33%.

**The interactions that decide it**
1. **Blade of the Exile (+3) lifts her small bodies out of Graves's range.** A Stranger with Blade is 5
   and survives a 4-damage blast; Punch First on Graves (9) fixes it.
2. **Graves's blast can be zeroed or dodged**: Switcheroo (83%) swaps his Might with a 0-Might Crab;
   Star-Crossed or Rebuke in response bounces him (a unit off the board reads null Might, §359.3.e.12,
   so no damage). Repulse counters any of them, since each chooses Graves.
3. **Astral Heron is always at a battlefield** (its discount needs it there), so Rebuke and Beast
   Below can reach it; Defy reaches neither (Rebuke costs 2 Power; Beast is a unit). Not So Fast can
   counter Beast's bounce. **Baron can't be chosen**: nothing in either version removes him; win by
   holding at 7 (the Baron Pit ruling).
4. **Her own Vex** stuns what you play while it's at a battlefield: a Graves or Beast Below arrives
   unable to move that turn (a defending Graves still blasts).
5. **Rampage is at its worst here**: Defy and Discipline in every list, Zhonya's in 93%.

**v1:** the plan in the guide: press her battlefields, Vex first, Rebuke the first Heron, attack with
two into her stuns, hold at 7 once the Pit is down. Its weak points are an early board it can't sweep
and one Rebuke for three Herons in game 1.
**v2:** the same plan, with **Graves** against her cheap board (defending, he blasts her attackers
before damage; attacking, with Punch First, he clears a battlefield) and **Beast Below** as a second
answer to Heron that also replays your Ezreal. It drops the Rampage that was weakest here.
*Costs:* a slightly slower turn 3, and two more big targets for her Star-Crossed and Rebuke.

**Reading: v2 is better placed.** Every change points at her deck: a sweeper for her small board, a
second Heron answer, and one fewer card her Calm protection blanks. **How sure:** medium. The best
evidence of any matchup, but no games.

**For the journal:** whether Graves's blast resolved or got Switcheroo'd or bounced; Heron turns (was
there an answer?); Baron games (did holding at 7 work?).

## Irelia, Blade Dancer (S) — Tempo · Movement

**Evidence: no one-on-one Radiance results yet** (her one listed result was the 2v2 event). Read from
**56 posted legal lists** since 16 Oct, so treat it as Vendetta-shaped and untested in Radiance.

**What she plays:** the legend readies a friendly unit when one of her spells chooses it (for 1
Power), and readies itself when she conquers.
- *Bodies, all small:* Stellacorn Herder ×3 (3, draws as it moves), Tideturner ×2.9 (2), Scuttle Crab
  ×2.8 (0), Irelia, Fervent (4, Deflect, grows when chosen or readied), Akali, Silent 64% (4; can't be
  chosen outside combat, +2 when she moves), her own Vex, Apathetic 61%, Pyke, Returned 45%.
- *Tricks, the densest in the field:* Defy ×2.9 and Discipline ×3 (98%), Defiant Dance ×2.9 (+2 to one,
  −2 to another, Reaction), En Garde, Twilight Shroud (can't be chosen this turn), Not So Fast 79%,
  **Flash 55%** (Reaction: move up to 2 of her units to base), Ride the Wind (move and ready), Charm.
- *Gear:* Boots of Swiftness ×2.4, Guardian Angel 79%, Zhonya's 73%.

**How she wins:** out-acting you. Every trick chooses her own unit, which readies it to go again;
Herder draws on every move. She wins fights by holding the last Reaction.

**The interactions that decide it**
1. **She has real escapes from Graves**: Flash moves two units home in response to his trigger;
   Defiant Dance gives him −2 (blast 2); Switcheroo (30%) zeroes it. Her units are small enough to die
   to the blast if it resolves.
2. **Not So Fast (79%) and Twilight Shroud** protect her key unit from Rebuke, Star-Crossed and Beast
   Below's bounce; Defy (98%) takes your Rampage, Star-Crossed and Sabotage.
3. **Her own Vex (61%)** stuns what you play.
4. **Your Power-paid tricks are what she can't Defy**: Punch First on your own unit is safe from Defy
   and Not So Fast both.

**v1:** a trick war. Your cheap bodies trade into her reactive pumps; the guide's plan is to press
and attack with two. Rampage is poor (Defy, Discipline, Zhonya's).
**v2:** Graves threatens her whole board but she has three ways to blunt the blast (Flash, Defiant
Dance, Switcheroo); Beast Below's bounce meets Not So Fast or Twilight Shroud. Two fewer 3-drops
matters against a tempo deck that wins on early efficiency.

**Reading: about the same, perhaps v1 slightly better.** v2's new cards are the kind her tricks are
best at blunting, and the slower turn 3 is a real cost against tempo. **How sure:** low. No Radiance
results, and the most trick-heavy matchup, where card reads predict least.

**For the journal:** did Graves's blast resolve, or did Flash or Defiant Dance answer it? Did turn 3
feel short?

## Kennen, Heart of the Tempest (S) — Midrange · Card flow

**Evidence:** 26 Radiance results, one event (the 25k), and the lists are near-identical: one room's
build, copied.

**What he plays:** the legend empowers whenever he plays a card from anywhere but his hand, then
spends it to give a unit Assault 2.
- *Bodies, mostly small:* Traveling Merchant ×3 (2), Trifarian Captain ×3 (1, digs 4 for a cheap unit or
  gear), Nocturne ×3 (4, Ganking; plays for 1 Power when seen off the top), Kennen, Storm of Shuriken
  (4), Fizz ×1.5 (3), Bedeviling Stranger 88%, Tideturner 65%, Innovator 62%, Ezreal 54%, Matriarch 46%.
- *Top end:* **Rhasa the Sunderer ×3** (6 Might; costs 1 less per card in his trash, so cheap late).
- *Dig and mana:* Lightning Rush ×3, Seal of Discord ×3 (Power), Last Rites ×1.9.
- *Answers:* **Switcheroo 100%** (×1.7), Star-Crossed 100%, Gust 96%, Flash 88%, **An Offer You Can't
  Refuse 77%**, Rebuke 50%, Hard Bargain 50%. **No Defy, no Discipline, no Zhonya's.**

**How he wins:** fill the trash, play from elsewhere for value and Assault, then Rhasa cheap.

**The interactions that decide it**
1. **No Defy, Discipline or Zhonya's**: your Rampage, Star-Crossed and Sabotage all resolve cleanly,
   and a Rampage kill readies your legend. **This is the matchup where Rampage is best**, so v2's cut
   costs the most here.
2. **Switcheroo in every list** zeroes Graves's blast; Star-Crossed and Rebuke bounce him in
   response; Flash (88%) moves two units home. Repulse counters the three that choose Graves.
3. **Offer (77%) kills your Edge of Night** (and gives you a Gold).
4. **Rhasa is a poor bounce target**: it costs him little to recast with a full trash, so Beast Below's
   bounce is worth less here than against Riven's Heron.
5. **Gust (96%)** reaches your 3-Might bodies (Ezreal, Fizz, Innovator) but not Graves (4) or Beast (8).

**v1:** the guide's plan: press early, before Rhasa is cheap; his trash is public, so count it. Three
clean Rampages are good removal here.
**v2:** Graves threatens his small board, but he has the most answers to it of any deck so far
(Switcheroo in every list, Star-Crossed, Flash). Beast Below's best target, a big unit, is the one he
recasts cheaply. One Rampage fewer and two fewer 3-drops both work against pressing early.

**Reading: v1 is slightly better placed.** Every v2 change is aimed at Calm decks (fewer kill spells,
more sweeping and bouncing), and Kennen is the opposite: no Calm protection, plenty of answers to a
sweeper, and a top end that shrugs off bounce. **How sure:** medium-low. Good numbers, but one room.

**For the journal:** Rampage kills (clean?), Graves against Switcheroo, and Rhasa's turn: were you
ahead by then?

## Evelynn, Agony's Embrace (A) — Tempo · Facedown

**Evidence:** 6 Radiance results, one event (the 25k); best #21 of 755.

**What she plays:** **your colours, and many of your cards.** The legend draws once a turn when she
plays a card from face down (discarding one if it's not her turn).
- *Hidden bodies:* Evelynn, Entrancing ×1.7, Pyke, Returned ×2.5 (Gold when your units die), Gutter
  Guardian ×2.8, Momentary Companion ×2.6 (4), Bedeviling Stranger 50%, Ember Monk 50% (+2 on face-down
  plays). *Open:* **her own Vex, Apathetic ×2.3**, K'Sante, Courageous 83% (4, Shield 2), Rengar, Trophy
  Hunter 83%, Kinkou Initiate 50%, Fizz and Ezreal 33–50%, Baron 33%.
- *Tricks and answers:* Switcheroo ×1.7, Star-Crossed ×1.7, Edge of Night ×1.7 (all 100%), Punch First
  83%, Rampage 83%, Hold 'Em 83% (+3), Onslaught 67% (+6), Gust 50%, Offer 33%, Existential Dread 33%.
  **No Defy, Discipline or Zhonya's.** Bandle Scouts' Academy (Gold for hiding) in every list.

**How she wins:** hiding at every battlefield she holds and playing from face down on either turn,
drawing as she does; your attacks into her battlefields meet whatever she flips.

**The interactions that decide it**
1. **Taking her battlefield trashes everything she hid there** (§323.6.a): the guide's "press her
   hide spots". Every conquest of hers is card advantage for you.
2. **Graves attacking is weak here; Graves defending is strong.** His blast only hits units present
   when he attacks or defends, and he deals no combat damage: attack her hide spot, and she flips a
   4-Might Momentary Companion or an Edge of Night (+2) after the blast, which Graves can't kill.
   Defending, he blasts her attackers, and she can't reinforce from face down at *your* battlefield
   (a hidden permanent must be played where it's hidden, §811.1.d.1).
3. **Her Vex** stuns what you play while it's at a battlefield, and her Switcheroo, Star-Crossed and
   Gust all chose; your Rebuke and **v2's Beast Below** can bounce her Vex (Deflect taxes 1 Power).
4. **With no Defy, Discipline or Zhonya's, Rampage is clean removal** against her, and a kill readies
   your legend.

**v1:** a near-mirror with her Hidden plan on top. Three Rampages and the guide's pressure on her hide
spots; two Tornados to hide behind your own holds.
**v2:** Graves holds your battlefields well against her attacks but attacks poorly into hers; Beast
Below bounces her Vex. One Rampage fewer, where Rampage is clean; two fewer 3-drops against a tempo
deck.

**Reading: about the same.** v2's gains (Beast on her Vex, Graves on defence) are offset by losses
that matter here (a clean Rampage, early tempo), and Graves's best use is the less important half of
this matchup. **How sure:** low-medium (6 results, one event).

**For the journal:** Graves's attacks into her battlefields (did a flip beat them?); her Vex: answered?

## Rengar, Pridestalker (A) — Midrange · Fights

**Evidence:** 10 Radiance results, one event; best #17 of 755. Also the **LA RQ winner** (Vendetta).

**What he plays:** the legend gives a unit +1 Might whenever he plays a unit.
- *Bodies, many and small:* Inferna ×3 (1, Ambush, Assault 2), Irresistible Faefolk ×3 (1; pulls one of
  your units to where it moves), Pit Rookie ×2.9 (2), First Mate ×2.1 (3), Grim Apothecary ×2.5 (3,
  Ambush), Kinkou Initiate ×2.8 (3), Nidalee, Cat Form ×2 (4, Ambush), Kai'Sa, Survivor ×3 (4), Noxus
  Hopeful ×3 (4, cheap with Legion), Pyke, Dockside Butcher 60%.
- *Top end:* Rengar, Trophy Hunter (6), Ferrous Forerunner 90% (6; leaves two 3-Might Mechs), Elnuk
  Herd 70% (5), **Graves 40%**, Darius 30%, **Brynhir Thundersong 30%** (you can't play cards that turn).
- *Tricks:* Punch First ×2.9, Thrill of the Hunt ×2.6 (Reaction: replays a unit to any battlefield),
  Rampage ×2.1, Sabotage ×1.6. **No Defy, Discipline, Zhonya's, Star-Crossed or bounce.**

**How he wins:** more bodies than you, growing as they land, arriving where it hurts (Ambush,
Thrill of the Hunt), card draw on half his units.

**The interactions that decide it**
1. **A board of small units**: 26 of his 30 are 4 Might or less. Graves's blast is at its best here;
   **Graves was already v1's sideboard plan against him.**
2. **But Ambush arrives after the blast**: Inferna, Apothecary, Nidalee and Rengar can be played into
   a fight as Reactions, and Graves can't kill what lands after his trigger. Defending, Graves blasts
   his attackers; he can still Ambush more in where he has units.
3. **Your Vex** stuns everything he plays while she's at a battlefield, and he has no bounce to remove
   her: one of the best Vex matchups.
4. **No Calm protection**: Rampage is clean against him, so v2's cut costs something.
5. **Brynhir (30%)** shuts off your Reactions for a turn: a fight you planned to win with Switcheroo
   or Star-Crossed fails that turn.

**v1:** Vex and fights; game 1 without Graves, which arrives from the sideboard in games 2 and 3.
**v2:** **Graves main for game 1**, the card you'd side in anyway; Beast Below bounces Forerunner or
Rengar; Vex as before. One clean Rampage fewer; two fewer 3-drops against the fastest board in the
field (16.1 Might by turn 4).

**Reading: v2 is better placed.** It plays game 1 the way v1 plays games 2 and 3. **How sure:**
medium (10 results plus a major win; the Ambush timing against Graves is the uncertainty).

**For the journal:** Graves defending against his attacks; Ambush units after the blast; whether
turn 3 kept up with his board.
