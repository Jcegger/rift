# Sivir scratch brew: every legal card, read against the identity

Written 27 Nov 2026 for `decklab/sivir-scratch.md` stage 6. Jay asked for "any and all cards available to us". The
pool is `rift pool "battle mistress" --since 2026-08-01 --all`: Body/Chaos/Colorless, unbanned, Standard, Sivir's own
signature only, never Aurora. That's 295 units, spells and gear, plus the 67 legal battlefields. Evidence: `siv
posted/good` = share of Sivir's posted lists and good finishes (5 good finishes, all late Sep to early Oct); then
proven here, crowd here, proven under another legend (named, with its good-finish share), or theory.

Verdicts: **IN** = in the draft; **CANDIDATE** = in contention for a job, decided at the draft; out = one-line reason.
Auto reasons (the out rules) come from the card's text; every IN and CANDIDATE was read and argued by hand.
