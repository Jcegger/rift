## 1. Identity — the reads (26 Oct 2026)

**The legend.** *Battle Mistress:* "When you recycle a rune, you may exhaust me to play a Gold
gear token exhausted. When one or more enemy units die, ready me." A Gold kills itself to add
one Power of any colour. **Gold is Power, never Energy.** It enters exhausted, so it pays for
next turn, not this one.

**The idea that makes it "Gold midrange": Treasure Trove.** 2 Energy gear: "When this leaves the
board, draw 1 and channel 1 rune exhausted. [Chaos], [Exhaust]: Kill this." Each Trove that
leaves is a card plus a permanent rune, so **Trove ramps Energy**, which Gold can't.
- **Pack of Wonders** (2 Energy gear: "[Exhaust]: Return another friendly gear, unit, or
  facedown card to its owner's hand") returns Trove to hand. **That counts as leaving the board,
  so Trove triggers** (§383.2.c.3, which uses Trove as its example). Replay it for 2 and do it
  again next turn: 2 Energy a turn for a card and a rune.
- The ramp is what pays for a real midgame: Graves (5), Rengar (5), Mercenary with Accelerate,
  and Mindsplitter (7) becomes castable.

**The evidence. No list of this shape has a record.** Three references, none of them a result:

| list | what it is | record |
|---|---|---|
| "Sivir - Battle Mistress Deck" (22 Oct, 108 views) | 3 Trove, 1 Pack, 2 Last Rites; 3 Staunch Survivor, 2 Graves; Fizz 2, Matriarch 2; 7 Body / 5 Chaos | none (posted a day before Radiance went legal) |
| "Sivir - RAD" (9 Oct, 19 views) | 2 Trove, 3 Pack, The Good Stuff; 2 Rengar, 1 Graves; Fizz 2, Matriarch 3 | none (a preview list) |
| NoddL's "Body Chaos" | 3 Trove, 3 Pack, 2 Mindsplitter, Rengar; heavy removal, so control | **5th and 6th** (event sizes not recorded), pre-Radiance; 3 Stacked Deck, now banned |

NoddL's placings are the only evidence that the Trove + Pack shell works. His list is the
control version, which is ruled out. The midrange version is a brew.

**Also tried before:** a "Gold-fuelled midrange" brew (20–21 Oct, `guides/sivir-gold-midrange-radiance.md`
in git history). It was built on Fizz and Matriarch plus the Hidden package, with no Trove,
and was retired on 23 Oct because its counts came from lists that were missing alt-art cards.
It isn't this deck.

**Lessons from the Randyyy games** (26 Oct), for stage 2:
- **Catch-up.** It needs a way to retake a battlefield late. Rengar, Trophy Hunter Ambushes onto
  a battlefield where only they have units (§822.1.d).
- **Midgame Might.** Its top end was 4 Energy, 4 Might.
- **Off-plan threats.** Blitzcrank (Vex lists, 14%) and a well-timed Star-Crossed both beat it.

## The choices for stage 1 (Jay's)

1. **How much trash engine stays?** Both midrange references still run 2 Fizz and 2–3 Matriarch.
   Keep Fizz only (the part that worked), keep both, or none?
2. **The top end.** Graves, Rengar, Mindsplitter, Sivir, Ambitious (6 Energy, 7 Might), or
   something else? The ramp decides how many it can afford.
3. **Start from a reference, or build up from the plan?** The 22 Oct list is the closest to
   "Gold midrange", but nothing has proven it. Starting from it and arguing every slot is how
   the Randyyy list was done.
4. **Anything else off the table,** beyond Dazzling Aurora?
