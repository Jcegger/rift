# Giving play advice

`docs/strategy.md` is how this repo chooses between two legal plays. Read it before
advising on a line, a mulligan, a matchup plan or a game review. Any rules premise the
advice stands on goes through the `rules-question` skill first.

1. **Terminal scan** (`docs/strategy.md` §0): can either player reach 8 before the other
   acts again? Your win first, then theirs. A hold at 7 wins, because hold points are not
   gated (§473.1.a.1). Future-position reasoning applies only after the scan comes up empty.
2. **Deck context**: for Jay's own deck, read its guide in `guides/` (mulligans, matchups,
   sideboarding); for the opponent, `docs/archetypes.md`.
3. **Choose the line** with the rest of `docs/strategy.md`, and give the verdict as a
   conditional: which line, why, and what would change your mind.
4. **Reviewing a game**: classify each mistake by the seven types in `docs/strategy.md` §8
   (terminal, state, objective, conversion, information, pressure, role), so the match
   journal's tallies stay comparable.
