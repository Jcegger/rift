## Batch 1: 13 October 2026

**Q1. Can Gust bounce a unit that was just flipped from Hidden?**

**Yes, if it has 3 Might or less**, and Gust is at its best exactly then. Gust is a
Reaction: *"return a unit at a battlefield with 3 Might or less to its owner's hand."* A
flipped unit is played from facedown onto its battlefield, so it's a unit at a battlefield
and a legal target.

The timing that matters: **cast Gust in response to the unit's "when you play me" effect.**
A unit played from Hidden must choose its targets at its own battlefield (§811.1.d.2), and
Riot's ruling is that this is read off the unit itself. Bounce the unit before its effect
resolves and **the effect loses its target and does nothing** (`docs/rules-rulings.md`,
the Kennen/Gust ruling). Played from hand, the same effect would still happen.

For you: this is why Evelynn and Tornado Warrior, both 2–3 Might, only get flipped into
tapped Chaos. Riven's deck has the same exposure on its 2–3 Might Hidden units.

**Q2. Why does going second get an extra rune?**

**To make up for moving second.** The first player acts first and can take a battlefield
first, so in a race to 8 they're a turn ahead. The rule: the player going second channels
an extra rune in their first Channel Phase (§485.7). That's their only compensation.

What it buys you: three runes on your first turn instead of two. That means a 3-drop on
turn 1, or a 2-drop with 1 Energy to spare. Plan your first turn around it when you choose
to go second.
