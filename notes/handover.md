## The plan: A is a trap

Hold one battlefield (A) with two units and a face-down card, make attacking it a mistake, pull their units into it,
and close by walking into the battlefield they left empty (B). A hold at 7 wins on its own. **One hold is enough:**
going for both battlefields is right only when the terminal scan says it wins that turn (game 2's lesson).

- **Closing turn (start on 6 holding A):** the hold makes 7 → Faefolk walks into A and pulls B's defender in (it
  attacks; they have focus first) → a face-down Stranger bounces it or Existential Dread stuns it → Sivir with
  Accelerate walks into the empty B → 8. Backups: a holder wearing Boots walks A → B (Ganking; their Vex only stuns
  units played that turn), Rengar by Ambush onto B, Ride the Wind.
- **Recital (after a loss):** every hold sends their lone B defender home; walk in and conquer: two points a turn.
- **The kill zone (Jay):** on your turn, Ember Monk into A, Faefolk pulls their unit (best: their Vex) into A, flip a
  face-down card (Monk +2), it dies, the kill readies the legend for a Gold.
- **Star-Crossed rebuy (Jay):** return your own Ezreal (draw 2 again), Innovator or Faefolk with their unit.
- **By archetype (guide §9, agreed 3 Nov):** default slow, one hold; **race Baron decks (before he lands) and Ramp.**
- **Never an Aurora deck; no Calm, no Kennen; cost and ownership are never arguments** (standing rules).

## Corrections to remember (each was said wrong at least once)

- **"When enemy units die, ready me" is the legend's (Battle Mistress) text, not Sivir's.** A kill readies the legend
  for a Gold; it never readies Sivir.
- **Kai'Sa kills a 6-Might Sivir with two cards:** Smoke Screen (−4, 97%) then Hextech Ray (3). Close with Unyielding
  Spirit in hand.
- **Rengar's Thrill of the Hunt (94%) replays a pulled unit into the emptied B and conquers it** on your turn.
- **The pull starts a fight at once, and they have focus first** (§323.12, §466.2.c.1.a). A surviving defender sends
  their unit to *their* base (§468.1.a.2), so B stays empty either way.
- **Vex, Apathetic stuns any unit the opponent plays while she's at a battlefield**, anywhere.
- **Ride the Wind is an Action, so it plays in their showdowns too:** it belongs in both camps (it reinforces A and
  can make Stranger's count of 4). It can't answer a spell on the chain (that takes a Reaction).
- **Battlefields are placed before turn order;** the previous game's loser picks the seat after seeing both.
- **Baron Nashor can't be chosen:** nothing in the deck pulls, bounces or kills him. Win by holding at 7.
- **A card played from face down ignores its whole cost** (§811.1.b). Hiding it costs one Power of any colour (Gold
  pays it).
- **A face-down Stranger bounces only at its own battlefield** (§811.1.d.3; the unit it returns is a target). From hand
  it can choose any battlefield, at main-phase speed, and in base it doesn't count toward the 4.
- **Existential Dread is Defy-able** (1E 1P). Defy counters ≤4 Energy and ≤1 Power.
- **Ezreal's discount on Sivir:** take the Energy, not the rune, or her 2 Power isn't spent (§445.1.b).
- **The test bench is blind to** pulls, bounces, face-down swaps, Ember Monk's trigger, Boots' Ganking, battlefields
  and gear draw. Read it for shape, not verdicts.
- **Hold-trigger battlefields serve whoever holds them:** fine for this deck now that it holds, risky in a blind pick.
