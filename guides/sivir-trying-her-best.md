## Executive summary: the game plan

*Read this before every round. Everything after it is detail.*

**What the deck is.** A Gold deck. Battle Mistress makes Power for free, so the deck is built from cards that are **cheap in Energy and paid in Power**. The trash engine (Fizz, Matriarch, Ezreal, Merchant) is how that Power turns into cards. **Gold pays for card advantage**: that is the identity.

**How it wins.** Take battlefields early with cheap bodies that don't fold. Lock one with Vex and face-down cards. Open the other with Evelynn's pull or Rebuke. Conquer it with Mercenary or a pumped unit for the eighth point. Every enemy death on the way readies the legend for another Gold.

**The resource rule.**
- **Energy** comes only from runes. On turns 2–4 it is the tight resource: developing and holding up tricks compete for it.
- **Power** comes from Gold and from recycling runes. It renews every turn, so it is the resource to lean on.
- **A Gold is next turn's Power.** It comes in exhausted and readies in your next Awaken. Gold you carry through your turn unspent is your Power on *their* turn.

**Every turn, in this order** (§3 has each step):
1. **Terminal scan.** Can either player reach 8 before the other acts again? A hold at 7 wins.
2. **Role.** Are you pressing or holding this game? The matchup decides it (§10).
3. **Count.** Runes ready, Gold ready, cards in hand. What does their open mana threaten?
4. **Choose: develop or hold up.** On turns 3–4 do one fully. Don't do half of each.
5. **Pump before you move.** Punch First goes on the attacker in your main phase.
6. **Move and take open battlefields.** Hide at every battlefield you hold.
7. **Fight.** Use Switcheroo on a unit at 4+ Might while they have Energy for Gust.
8. **End of turn.** Spend Gold on your turn, or keep it ready on purpose for theirs.

**The plan by stage.**

| stage | job | the play |
|---|---|---|
| turn 1 (2 Energy) | presence | a 2-drop: Merchant, Evelynn, or Staunch with a recycled rune |
| turn 2 (4 Energy) | develop | a 3-drop and a 1, or two 2-drops; start hiding |
| turn 3 (6 Energy) | **two plays** | two 3-drops; or Matriarch plus her Empower (a turn-3 revive) if the trash has a unit; or Sivir and a 2 |
| turn 4 (8 Energy) | lock a battlefield | Vex at a battlefield; Rengar plus a 3; Edge on a small unit |
| turn 5+ | grind and close | Fizz and Matriarch rebuild; Evelynn opens their battlefield; Mercenary or Rengar takes it |

**The five rules that matter most.**
1. **Hold at 7 and win.** Hold points are not gated (§473.1.a.1). The eighth point by **conquer** needs both battlefields scored that turn (§473.1.b): hold one, then conquer the other.
2. **Gold first, runes for the rest.** One recycle a turn makes a Gold while the legend is ready, and an enemy death readies it again: **recycle, kill, recycle.**
3. **Hide every turn you can,** and know which card to hide (§3.4). A hidden card costs a Gold now and nothing later.
4. **Small bodies hold and loot; they don't trade.** Make them sturdy with Edge of Night and Staunch, or fight with a face-down card ready.
5. **Switcheroo on a unit at 4+ Might** while they have Energy up. Gust bounces a unit at 3 or less in response, and the swap fails (§359.3.e.12).

**How the matchup changes the plan** (§10 has every opponent):

| role | against | why |
|---|---|---|
| **press early** | Kennen, Kai'Sa, Azir, Jayce, Ziggs, Irelia | they are slower than you (fewer units by turn 3) or get stronger late |
| **hold** | Riven, Rengar, LeBlanc, Rek'Sai, Evelynn's hide spots | they are as fast or faster, or punish a spread-out attack |
| **attack with two** | Master Yi | his +2 is only for a lone defender |

## 2. How it got here

The deck started as Randyyy's list with seven changes (25 Oct) and was rebuilt over the next two nights. What changed, and why:

| change | why |
|---|---|
| +3 Reckless Innovator (for Last Rites, a Tornado, a Matriarch) | Gold was piling up and hands ran dry; Innovator turns Gold into cards |
| +2 Vex, Apathetic (for Pyke, an Edge) | stuns everything they play while she's at a battlefield; cancels Riven's "enters ready" |
| +2 Staunch Survivor (for a Shadow Order Disciple, Acceptable Losses) | Radiance's best 2-drop: attacks and defends at 4. Acceptable Losses moved to the sideboard (gear hate) |
| −1 Tideturner, −2 Staunch Survivor, +1 Matriarch, +2 Rengar (Jay's own edit) | a finisher; Rengar's Ambush answers being out-sized. Staunch fed neither Fizz nor Matriarch |
| −2 Shadow Order Disciple, +2 Staunch Survivor (back in) | **the rule that defines the deck:** cheap in Energy and paid in Power beats cheap in both. Disciple costs no Power, so he leaves Gold idle |
| −1 Gust, +1 Edge of Night (2 now) | a pump paid in Power (hide for a Gold, re-equip for a Chaos) that also keeps 2-drops out of ping range |
| −1 Grim Resolve, +1 Rebuke (swapped with the sideboard) | paid in Power; bounces any unit at a battlefield; Fizz can recast it; can't be Defied |
| −1 Tideturner, +1 Evelynn, Entrancing | the only card that opens *their* battlefield for the eighth point |
| sideboard: −Acceptable Losses, −Grim Resolve, +1 Rebuke, +1 Repulse (28 Oct) | **the Riven plan** (§10): Rebuke is the clean answer to Astral Heron (91% of her tournament lists); Repulse protects Vex and a pumped attacker from her stuns and bounces. Offer already covered gear |

**The premise that turned out wrong.** The early plan treated turn-1 units as essential, because Randyyy had 8 of them. Jay's games never showed a turn-1 problem. The goldfish agrees: the deck always has a play on turns 1–2.

**Tried and rejected, so they don't come back without a new reason:**
- **Ramp** (Treasure Trove, Mobilize): there was nothing to ramp into.
- **Black Market:** 2 Power a card. Revisit only if the late game runs dry (§13).
- **Rhasa:** Jay doesn't like it.
- **Mindsplitter:** too slow without ramp, and a hand-attack deck feels mean at a casual night.
- **Onslaught:** 4 Energy and no Power, so it fails the rule.
- **Kraken Hunter:** its cost cut needs buffs.
- **Elnuk Herd:** from face down it's a one-shot +3.
- **Graves in the main deck:** he only matters against some decks, so he's in the sideboard.

## 3. Sequencing

The deck wins and loses on order more than on cards. This section is the long version of the executive summary's eight steps.

### 3.1 The terminal scan comes first

Before anything else, ask whether the game can end before anyone gets another turn ([strategy.md §0](../docs/strategy.md#0-the-order-of-every-decision)). First look for your win, then for theirs.

| you are at | and you | result |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | hold a battlefield at the start of your turn | **you win** (§473.1.a.1) |
| 7 | conquer, having scored the other battlefield this turn | you win |
| 7 | conquer, *not* having scored the other this turn | you draw a card instead (§473.1.b.1) |
| 6 | hold two at the start of your turn | **you win**: two hold points |
| 6 | hold one, then conquer the other | you win |
| 6 | hold nothing, conquer both | you win |

**Read it from their side.** An opponent at 7 holding a battlefield wins at the start of their turn unless you take it now. That outranks every plan you had.

**The Kennen game (26 Oct) was this table.** You were at 6, controlling both battlefields, on his turn. If you survived it with both, the two holds won. Everything that turn was about keeping the weaker battlefield, not about value.

### 3.2 Paying costs

**Runes do both jobs.** Each rune can be **exhausted for 1 Energy and then recycled for 1 Power of its colour, in the same turn** (§164.2). Recycling doesn't need the rune ready. What it costs is the rune: it goes to the bottom of your rune deck, and next turn's board is one smaller. You channel 2 a turn (§315.3.b). To cast something costing E Energy and P Power, you need max(E, P) runes on the board, with E of them ready ([rules.md](../docs/rules.md), Resources).

**Gold.**
- **The legend.** When you recycle a rune, you may exhaust Battle Mistress to play a **Gold gear token, exhausted**. When one or more enemy units die, she readies.
- **Innovator** makes Gold too.
- **Spending it.** A Gold's ability is "**Reaction**: kill this, exhaust: add one Power of any colour". It comes in exhausted and readies in your next Awaken (§315.1.b). So:
  - **a Gold you make this turn is Power for your next turn;**
  - **a ready Gold you don't spend on your turn is Power on theirs.** Kept on purpose, it pays for Punch First, Switcheroo or Rebuke in a fight on their turn, or Star-Crossed at any time.

**The order to pay in.**
1. **Ready Gold first.** It's free.
2. **Then recycle runes you've already tapped for Energy.** That costs only next turn's board.
3. **Make your one Gold a turn.** The first recycle while the legend is ready makes it.
4. **Kill something, then recycle again.** An enemy death readies the legend, so the next recycle makes another Gold: **recycle, kill, recycle.**

**Early turns are the exception.** On turns 1–3 every recycle slows your curve, so lean on Gold and accept a 1-Power card waiting a turn. From about turn 5, your board has more runes than your Energy needs (the deck's biggest play is about 6 Energy), and the extra ones are a Power bank.

**Gold pays any colour,** so it covers Punch First's second Body and Matriarch's Chaos. Pay coloured costs from the matching rune and leave the Gold flexible.

### 3.3 Develop or hold up: the turn 3–4 decision

The engine model (`scripts/rift try`, with hiding and opponents that apply pressure) found the deck's one structural tension. **About 1.8 tricks a game go uncast because the Energy went to developing**, and 3.4 a game under heavy pressure. Cheaper units didn't change that in testing. Cheaper tricks helped a little, at a cost. **It's a sequencing problem, so solve it in sequencing:**

- **Each turn, decide which kind of turn it is.**
  - **Developing:** tap out for two plays.
  - **Holding up:** one play, with Energy left for the trick you expect to need.
  - Half of each usually gets you a weak board *and* an uncastable trick.
- **What holding up needs:**
  - **Punch First:** 1 untapped rune plus 2 ready Gold or 2 recycles.
  - **Switcheroo or Rebuke from hand:** 2 runes plus 2 Power.
  - **Star-Crossed:** 3 runes plus 1 Power.
- **Hide Switcheroo a turn early** if you'll want it on their turn. From face down it costs nothing, Power included.
- **Keep Gold ready over their turn** when a fight is likely. That's Power that doesn't cost a rune.
- **When to develop:** when you're behind on board, or when they have no fight to start.
- **When to hold up:** when you're ahead on board and they need to attack to catch up. That's usually right after you take a battlefield.

### 3.4 Hidden: what to hide, and where

**The rules** (§811):
- **When and where:** pay 1 Power (Gold works) during your turn to put the card face down at **a battlefield you control**. One per battlefield (§811.1.b).
- **Playing it later:** from the next turn on, it has Reaction and is played **ignoring its whole cost, Power included**. A hidden unit or gear has to be played to that battlefield (§811.1.d.1).
- **Losing the battlefield:** if you lose control of it, the face-down card is trashed, not returned (§323.6.a).

**You'll have more Hidden cards than slots.** With 7 Hidden cards and at most 2 battlefields, hide in this order:
1. **Evelynn, the turn before you want to open their battlefield.** It's your closing move (§3.7).
2. **Switcheroo, when you expect to defend against a big attacker.** From face down it's a free Reaction.
3. **Edge of Night, when a surprise +2 in a fight matters.** Otherwise play it from hand and equip it.
4. **Tornado Warrior, at the battlefield where Matriarch is.** Its flip empowers her for free (§5).

**Hide only behind a hold that's hard to break.** Losing the battlefield costs you the card as well.

### 3.5 Fights

- **Pump before you move.** Punch First on your attacker in your main phase, then move. Once combat starts, only Reactions can answer triggers.
- **The attacker must kill every defender,** or the attackers are recalled and it's No Result (§468.1.a, §468.3.d). Don't attack into a hold you can't clear.
- **A mutual wipe leaves the battlefield uncontrolled** (§468.5.b). If they controlled it, an even trade costs them the hold, which is often worth the cards.
- **Assume their face-down card is a trick.** Switcheroo is in 82% of Kennen lists, Hidden Blade in 84% of LeBlanc lists and 86% of Azir lists. Bait it with something small before committing your biggest pump.
- **Switcheroo:**
  - Swap a small unit of yours with their big one, but **use a unit at 4+ Might while they have Energy for Gust.** Staunch counts: he's 4 when attacking or defending. So does any unit wearing Edge.
  - If they bounce or kill either unit in response, no Might moves. Once it resolves, your unit keeps the swap for the turn ([rules-rulings.md](../docs/rules-rulings.md), Switcheroo).
- **Rampage is not a fight trick.** It has no Action, so it's your main phase only. Pay its Body for the +2; Ezreal's discount can cover it. Both units deal damage equal to their Might to each other, so your unit has to survive it.
- **Mercenary needs 2 Power spent this turn.**
  - Accelerating her pays that by itself: her 1 plus Accelerate's Chaos. Ezreal's discount can drop that to 1, so check.
  - Any 2-Power card also does it: Punch First, Switcheroo or Rebuke.
  - She's then 6 Might with Ganking (11 with Punch First).

### 3.6 The engine

**Filling the trash:**
- **Traveling Merchant:** when he moves, discard 1, then draw 1.
- **Ezreal:** when played, discard 1, then draw 2.
- **Zaun Warrens:** when you conquer it, discard 1, then draw 1.
- **Spells you cast and units that die** also go to the trash.

**Discard on purpose.**
- **Fizz coming:** discard a spell of 3 Energy or less. All 13 main-deck spells qualify.
- **Matriarch coming:** discard a unit of 3 Energy or less with 1 Power or less: Merchant, Evelynn, Staunch, Innovator, Fizz, Ezreal or Tornado. 17 cards qualify.
- **Otherwise:** discard the card least likely to matter this game.

**Fizz** (3 Energy, 1 Power): when played, cast a spell from your trash costing 3 Energy or less, ignoring its Energy but paying its Power, then recycle it.
- **Best targets:** Punch First on an attacker before combat (2 Power, so Gold), Rebuke (2 Power) and Rampage (free; pay its Body for the +2).
- **The model** finds him a target about 75% of the time.

**Tail-Cloaked Matriarch** (4 Energy): her Empower costs 2 Energy and a Chaos. When she becomes Empowered, play a unit from your trash with Energy 3 or less and Power 1 or less **to your base, ignoring its cost.**
- **Best revives:** Ezreal (2 more cards), Fizz (a spell back) and Innovator.
- **Empowered is permanent,** so she normally revives once (§443).
- **Two ways to revive again:**
  - **Tornado Warrior** flipped at her battlefield empowers her free, then disempowers her at end of turn, so she can be empowered again later. Whether that works when she's already Empowered is an open question (§12).
  - **Star-Crossed** returns her to your hand to replay.
- **The turn-3 line:** 6 runes pay for Matriarch and her Empower, if Merchant or Ezreal put a unit in the trash on turn 2.

**Star-Crossed** (Reaction, 3 Energy, 1 Power) returns one of your units *and* one of theirs to hand. Pick a used Matriarch, Ezreal or Fizz of yours, to replay for value.

**Reckless Innovator** (3 Energy; Accelerate):
- **His trigger:** when he becomes exhausted, choose one: play a Gold, exhausted; or pay a Chaos to draw 1. Gold can pay that Chaos.
- **What exhausts him:** moving or attacking. Accelerate makes him enter ready, and entering ready isn't exhausting, so it doesn't trigger him.
- **The choice:** draw when your hand is thin or Gold is piling up; take the Gold when you're short of Power.

**The engine in a real game.** Against Kennen, Matriarch was empowered, revived Ezreal, and Ezreal drew Punch First.

### 3.7 Closing lines

1. **Hold one, open the other with Evelynn.**
   - Hide Evelynn at a battlefield you hold. On your next turn, flip her and pull their lone unit from the other battlefield to hers.
   - A player who has no units at a battlefield loses control of it at the next cleanup (§323.5), and their face-down card there is trashed (§323.6.a).
   - Their battlefield is now open. Move in and conquer it. You held one at the start of the turn, so this conquest scores.
   - Their pulled unit lands in a fight on your battlefield, where your hold, your face-down card and Staunch's Shield are.
2. **Rebuke the lone holder.** Same idea, from hand, in your main phase. Defy can't counter it.
3. **Mercenary breaks it.** Accelerate her, or spend 2 Power first, and she's 6 Might with Ganking. Punch First makes her 11. She closes best with a partner beside her.
4. **Rengar ambushes** a battlefield where you have units, as a Reaction. He can also be played to a battlefield with enemy units even if you have none there.

**Turn 1 (2 Energy; 3 going second, §487.7).**
- **Play a 2-drop.** Merchant or Evelynn cost no Power. Staunch needs one recycle, which also makes your first Gold.
- **Evelynn from hand is just a 2-Might body.** Her pull works only from face down, but that's fine on turn 1.
- **No 2-drop?** Pass with Energy and Power open. Nothing in your deck punishes it.

**Turn 2 (4 Energy).**
- **Play** a 3-drop plus a 1 (Sabotage), or two 2-drops.
- **Start hiding** at the battlefield you hold.
- **Ezreal now** sets up a turn-3 Matriarch: discard a unit.

**Turn 3 (6 Energy).**
- **This is the deck's best turn:** two 3-drops (Innovator and Fizz or Ezreal).
- **Or** Matriarch plus her Empower, if the trash has a unit worth reviving.
- **Or** Sivir plus a 2.

**Turn 4 (8 Energy).**
- **Put Vex at a battlefield.**
- **Or** Rengar plus a 3, or a 4 and a 3 with a rune spare for Punch First.
- **Put Edge on a small unit** that's holding.

**Turn 5 and later.**
- **Energy stops being the limit.** Recycle the extra runes for Power and spend it.
- **Hands are what run out.** Innovator's draws, Ezreal, Fizz and Matriarch are what refill them.
- **Look for the closing line** (§3.7).

## 5. Card by card

### The legend and the champion
- **Battle Mistress.** When you recycle a rune, you may exhaust her to play an exhausted Gold. When one or more enemy units die, she readies. **Every kill is a Gold.** That includes kills on their turn: a defender that wins its fight readies her for a recycle then.
- **Sivir, Mercenary** (4 Energy, 1 Power, Chaos; Champion Zone).
  - **Accelerate** costs 1 Energy and a Chaos.
  - **Switched on** once you've spent 2 Power this turn: +2 Might (6) and Ganking.
  - **She's always available,** so you always have a 4-drop. Save her for the turn she switches on, unless the board needs a body now.

### Early bodies (taking battlefields)
- **Traveling Merchant ×3** (2 Energy). Loots each time he moves. He's 2 Might, so don't trade him.
- **Evelynn, Entrancing ×1** (2 Energy; Hidden; Backline). From face down on your turn, she moves an enemy unit at a different location (their base included) to her battlefield. Backline means she takes combat damage last.
- **Staunch Survivor ×2** (2 Energy, 1 Body; Assault 2, Shield 2). Attacks and defends at 4, and **with Edge he's 6.** Outside a fight he's 2, so pings and Gust still hit him.

### The engine
- **Reckless Innovator ×3**, **Fizz ×3**, **Ezreal ×3**, **Tail-Cloaked Matriarch ×3**, **Tornado Warrior ×2** (3 Energy; Hidden). Text and lines in §3.6.
  - Ezreal's discount ("optional additional costs you pay cost 1 Energy or 1 Power less") covers Accelerate on Innovator and Sivir and Rampage's Body.
  - Tornado is mostly a 3-Might body you hide for a Gold and flip for free.

### Holding
- **Vex, Apathetic ×2** (4 Energy; Deflect). When an opponent plays a unit while she's at a battlefield, stun it, and they can't move it this turn.
  - **Any battlefield counts,** and units played to their base are stunned too: Matriarch revives, Nocturne off the top of their deck.
  - **A stunned unit deals no combat damage this turn,** which cancels Riven's "enters ready".
  - **Keep her at a battlefield.**

### Finishers
- **Rengar, Trophy Hunter ×2** (5 Energy, 1 Body; 6 Might; Ambush). He's the answer to being out-sized. Matriarch can't revive him: he costs more than 3.

### Tricks paid in Power
- **Punch First ×3** (1 Energy, 2 Body; Action). +5 this turn. Switches Mercenary on by itself. Defy can't counter it.
- **Switcheroo ×2** (2 Energy, 2 Chaos; Hidden; Action). Swap the Might of two units at the same battlefield this turn. Best hidden. Read §3.5 before using it.
- **Rebuke ×1** (2 Energy, 2 Chaos; Action). Return a unit at a battlefield to its owner's hand. Any size; Defy can't counter it. Fizz can recast it.
- **Edge of Night ×2** (3 Energy; Hidden gear; Equip: a Chaos).
  - **From face down** it attaches to a unit of yours there for +2 while attached.
  - **It outlives the unit:** unattached gear at a battlefield is recalled to your base (§149.3, §323.6), ready to equip again for a Chaos, which Gold can pay.
  - **The only real answer to pings** in the main deck.

### Removal and denial
- **Rampage ×3** (3 Energy; optional Body for +2). Your main phase only. Choose your unit and theirs; they damage each other by Might. A kill readies the legend.
- **Star-Crossed ×2** (3 Energy, 1 Chaos; Reaction). Returns one of theirs and one of yours. Defy *can* counter it.
- **Sabotage ×2** (1 Energy, 1 Body; your main phase). They reveal their hand; you pick a non-unit card and they recycle it. It strips the trick they were saving. Defy can counter it. **Against removal decks keep it in**: it's the one card that takes their removal before it's cast (Jay, 29 Oct: a Viktor game won on it, and it takes Kai'Sa's Time Warp). Against the Calm decks, whose Defy reaches it, it can still come out.

### Battlefields
- **Zaun Warrens:** discard 1, then draw 1 on conquer, which feeds the trash. **Not against Kennen,** who runs it in 13 of 16 lists and uses the loot better.
- **Sigil of the Storm:** when you conquer here, you must recycle one of your runes.
  - **It probably pays you.** Your legend triggers on "when you recycle a rune", with no restriction, so a Sigil recycle while she's ready should give you a Gold.
  - **For them it's just a rune lost.** That reading combines two cards and isn't covered by a ruling (§12).
  - **The old reason still holds:** going second you have the extra rune (§487.7).
- **Sandswept Tomb:** each spell that chooses friendly units here costs 1 Power less. The deck spends a lot of Power, so Punch First, Switcheroo and Rebuke on your unit there get cheaper.

**Which to bring.**
- **Game 1:** Zaun Warrens, except against Kennen.
- **Going second:** Sigil of the Storm.
- **Going first:** Sandswept Tomb.
- **A battlefield used in a decided game is retired for the match** (§488.5).
- **Avoid fighting on their power battlefields:** Void Gate (+1 to damage aimed at units there) and Rockfall Path (no units played there, so your hidden *units* can't flip; hide a spell).

## 6. Mulligans

The goldfish's best policy is **keep all**: no rule it tried spends more Energy over turns 1–4.

**By card,** measured against bottoming it:
- **Keep first:** Innovator, Merchant, Evelynn, Matriarch, Vex.
- **Bottom first:** Sabotage, Punch First, Switcheroo, Staunch.

**By hand:**
- **Keep** a 2-drop plus a 3-drop.
- **Keep** two 3-drops; turn 2 can be a 3 and a 1.
- **Ship** a hand of only tricks, or one with no play before turn 3.

**Against the press-early matchups,** keep the body-heavy hand over the trick-heavy one.

## 7. Power and Energy, by the numbers

**Colour demand.**
- **Body (5 runes):** 3 Punch First (2 each), 2 Staunch, 2 Rengar, 2 Sabotage, and Rampage's optional Body.
- **Chaos (7 runes):** 2 Switcheroo (2 each), Rebuke (2), 3 Fizz, 3 Ezreal, 2 Star-Crossed, Sivir, plus Matriarch's Empower, Edge's re-equip and Innovator's Accelerate and draw.
- **The split matches.** That's about 56% Chaos, and 7 of 12 runes is 58%.
- **Punch First's 2 Body is the one tight cost.** Gold covers the second.

**Power per turn, mid-game, roughly:**
- **Supply:** about 2 from recycled runes at a steady board, 1 Gold from the legend, 1 from Innovator if he's out, and 1 more for each turn with an enemy death.
- **Demand on a typical turn:** a 1-Power unit, a hide and a Punch First, which is about 4.
- **They match,** which is why Gold got spent against Kennen and felt "clutch".
- **No card costs more than 2 Power,** so you never have to save Power for one big play. You can still bank Gold for a big turn.

**Energy spent per turn,** in the quick goldfish (no engine, tricks held):
- **On the play:** 1.3 / 3.6 / 5.3 / 6.6 / 6.5 (turns 1–5).
- **On the draw:** 2.9 / 4.5 / 6.0 / 6.7 / 4.6.

The engine model (§3.3) is the better guide to the real game: Energy is tight on turns 2–4 and opens up after.

**Engine model, normal pressure:**
- **Fizz** finds a spell 75% of the time.
- **Matriarch** revives about 1.0 unit a game.
- **About 3.2 extra cards drawn** a game.
- **Mercenary is on** in 52% of turns 4–8.
- **About 4.2 Gold** a game over eight turns.

None of these are win rates. The model can't rank decks ([README](../README.md), the points model), and it doesn't play Evelynn's pull.

### Against Riven, in detail (28 Oct 2026)

She is the most-played deck in the field (89 of 701 lists at the LDXP Sacramento 25k) and her tournament lists (46 results) are what this is built on. A plan from the cards, not yet tested: log every Riven game.

1. **Press, and deny her battlefields.** The Exile's whole engine, units entering ready, works only on battlefields she controls. Contest them early; you are bigger by turn 4 (15.9 Might against her 13.7). Her late game (Heron into Baron) is better than yours, so quiet turns are hers (strategy.md §2).
2. **Vex, Apathetic is the card.** At a battlefield she stuns every unit Riven plays, and they can't move that turn, which cancels "enter ready". Riven answers her with Star-Crossed or Rebuke, each paying 1 more Power for Deflect; **Repulse** counters either.
3. **Rebuke the first Astral Heron the turn after it lands.** Heron discounts only while at a battlefield, so it is always where you can reach it, and 2 Energy for her 7 is the best trade in the matchup; Defy can't touch Rebuke. Do it before the discount pays for Baron (10 Energy 3 Power becomes 8 and 1). Switcheroo in a fight there is the backup.
4. **Don't pump one attacker into a stun.** Her Riven, Broken Blade stuns when she becomes exhausted, and Ki Burst (33% of her results) is an Action that needs her to exhaust a ready unit. A stunned unit deals no combat damage, so a lone pumped attacker can lose the fight and the 2 Power. Attack with two, or hold Repulse for the stun.
5. **If Baron lands, stop conquering for the win.** His Baron Pit is a third battlefield, and the final point by conquer needs every battlefield scored that turn: hold one, conquer the other at 7, and you only draw a card. **A hold at 7 still wins** (§473.1.a.1; docs/rules-rulings.md, "The Baron Pit is a battlefield"). Hold your two and outscore the Pit, 2 a turn to 1. Nothing in the deck can choose Baron.
6. **Rampage is the worst card here:** Defy counters it, Discipline (in every list) flips the damage trade as a Reaction, and Zhonya's turns the kill into a recall, so the legend doesn't ready. Sabotage stays: before a fight it shows her hand and strips a Discipline, Defy or Blade of the Exile, and if she Defies it she has traded her counter for your 1-drop.
- **Kennen:** be ahead before Rhasa is cheap. His trash is public, so count it.
- **Irelia:** spend only Power-paid tricks into her.
- **Master Yi:** never let him defend alone and win.

## 12. Open questions for a judge

1. **Sigil of the Storm and the legend.** Does Sigil's forced recycle trigger Battle Mistress's "when you recycle a rune"? The text has no restriction, so this guide reads yes. There's no ruling.
2. **Tornado Warrior on an already-Empowered Matriarch.** Does the flip's end-of-turn disempower still reset her? This guide reads yes (§442.1.c, §359.3.e.14.b). There's no ruling.
3. **Mercenary on the opponent's turn.** "This turn" reads as whichever turn it is, so 2 Power spent on their turn makes her a 6-Might defender. That's plain on the card but not ruled.
4. **Switcheroo when a unit leaves in response** is derived in this repo, not ruled ([rules-rulings.md](../docs/rules-rulings.md)). It matched what happened at the table on 25 October.

## 13. What to log, and what changes next

**Log every game in the app journal** (one tap: opponent, W or L). Add a line on *how* it was won or lost. The record says little until about ten games; the notes say more.

**The questions:**
1. **Gold:** spent every turn, or short? If short, the Power load went too far.
2. **Develop or hold up:** did you skip a trick because the Energy went to developing?
3. **Late game:** on turns 5 and later, runes left and nothing to play?
4. **Edge of Night and Staunch:** did your small bodies survive pings?
5. **The third Matriarch:** a good revive target on turns 3–5, or a 4-Energy body?
6. **Evelynn:** did she open a battlefield? Did you have a slot to hide her?
7. **Losses:** classify each with the seven types (§11).
