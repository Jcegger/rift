**The plan.** Hold one battlefield (**A**) and make attacking it a mistake. Pull their units into A. Sivir walks into the battlefield they left empty (**B**). A hold at 7 wins on its own.

**The closing turn** (start on 6, holding A, Faefolk in base):

1. Hold A → **7**.
2. **Faefolk** walks base → A and pulls B's defender into A. A fight starts at once: **they attack, with focus first.**
3. When focus reaches you, flip **Stranger** (your units + Faefolk + theirs + Stranger = 4) and bounce it. **Don't pass with focus before you flip.** No Stranger? **Existential Dread** stuns the pulled unit, or win the fight (Punch First, Switcheroo): if any defender survives, their unit goes to *their base*, so B stays empty.
4. **Sivir** with Accelerate: enters ready, 6 Might. Once A's fight is over, walk base → empty B → **8**.

**On their turn, they attack A:** 2+ attackers → **Stranger** · 1-on-1 → **Existential Dread** (stun; Repeat bounces), **Switcheroo**, **Edge of Night** (+2 for good) or **Gust** · one big attacker → **Nidalee / Rengar by Ambush**, or **Tideturner** swaps a big unit in · a flood → **Graves** (sideboard).

**The kill zone (your turn):** Ember Monk into A · Faefolk pulls their unit (best: their **Vex**) into A · flip your face-down card (Monk +2) · it dies, legend readies. They act first; count their Energy for Ambush.

**Four rules.**

1. From 5 points, every turn: can either player reach 8 before the other acts again?
2. A is the whole game. Losing it trashes your face-down card there.
3. **Their Vex, Apathetic is gone before Sivir** (she stuns any unit you play, anywhere). Rebuke her on the closing turn: 3 Power with Deflect turns Sivir on.
4. Keep a Faefolk in base from turn 4.

**Face-down.** One per battlefield you hold; hide on your turn (pay with Gold); free with Reaction from the next turn, theirs too. **Stranger needs 4 units, itself included: not for 1-on-1s. Face down it bounces only at its own battlefield (§811.1.d.3).** Only one card fits at A: choose by the fight you expect (Stranger, Switcheroo, Edge, Tideturner, Evelynn); **Ember Monk** at A gets +2 on any flip.

**Breaks the close:** their Vex · Gust or Star-Crossed on Stranger · Not So Fast · Moonfall / Hidden Blade / Rebuke on Sivir in B · **Baron** (can't be pulled or bounced; with the Pit out, **win by holding at 7**).

**Against Defy.** Safe: Punch First, Rebuke, Switcheroo, every unit. Counterable: Ride the Wind, Gust, Rampage, Star-Crossed, Existential Dread.

**Star-Crossed rebuy:** return your own Ezreal (draw 2 again), Innovator or Faefolk with their unit.

**Watch out.** Ezreal on Sivir: take the **Energy**, not the rune. Never make **Rockfall Path** your A. Equip Boots or play Ember Monk only on a turn you don't need Energy up. **Boots' Ganking:** a holder walks A → B, past their Vex.

**Battlefields** (picked before the seat): game 1 **Sigil of the Storm** · after a loss (go first) **Amateur Recital**: make it A; each hold sends their lone B defender home, walk in, two points a turn; if they take it, retake it first · after a win **Forbidding Waste** (hold it with two).

**Mulligan.** Want two two-drops and a 3-drop. Send back Rengar, Rampage, Star-Crossed, Boots first.

**By archetype.** Default slow: one hold, let them come to A. **Race Baron decks and Ramp.** Aggro: defend, then hold. Tempo: make them spend first. Control: they won't attack A, so pull and race to 7.

**Log each game:** ran out of cards? (turn, and was Innovator alive?) · couldn't reach 6 · couldn't open a battlefield · missed the second conquest · Sivir got answered · **did the trap fire?**
