## The plan in five lines

1. **Hold early.** Take the open battlefields with cheap bodies and hold each with two bodies and a hidden card. Holding is scoring: a point a battlefield every turn.
2. **Evelynn is the clock.** *When she holds, if you control a facedown card there, score 1 point.* A held battlefield with Evelynn and a hide pays **2 a turn**. Her point is gaining points, so the 8th-point rule never blocks it (§470.2, §473.1.a.1): **at 6, a held battlefield with Evelynn and a hide wins at the start of your turn.**
3. **The hide at her battlefield has to survive their turn unflipped** (the condition is checked when she holds, §383.2.a.1). Defend her battlefield with bodies; flip the hide at the other one.
4. **Bandle Tree holds two facedown cards** for whoever controls it (§190.6.d): keep one for Evelynn, flip the other. Losing it trashes both (§323.6.a), so hide the second only when the hold is safe.
5. **Go tall later.** From the midgame, put everything into one threat that breaks their last hold: Mercenary (Accelerate makes her enter ready at 6 Might with Ganking, since that pays two Power), Graves, or a unit wearing Edge of Night. Punch First on it before it moves.

## The clock (50-minute rounds)

- About **16 minutes a game** if all three are played.
- **At time** the active player finishes the turn, then **three more turns**; a game still on is won only by a player **2 or more points ahead**, otherwise it's a draw. **No new game starts** between games (TR §408.2). A drawn match is not a 3-0.
- If game 2 starts past the 30-minute mark, a third game probably won't finish. Play game 2 to win it outright.
- When time is called, count the three turns: who gets the last one, and can you be 2 ahead at the end of it?
- **Think on their turn.** Your own turn should be execution.

## Your turn, in order

0. **Lethal check, both players.** Are you at 6 with Evelynn and a hide on a held battlefield? Are they at 7 and holding?
1. **Count Power:** ready Gold, runes by colour. One rune recycled a turn makes the next Gold; pay the rest with Gold.
2. **Take open battlefields first.**
3. **Buff before you move** into a fight.
4. **Develop:** bodies to the battlefields you hold.
5. **Hide** at battlefields you control and will keep. At Evelynn's battlefield, hide a card you won't need to flip.
6. **Fight.**

## Rules that catch people

- **Rampage has no Action:** your turn only. Its optional Body gives +2 first, and Ezreal makes that free.
- **Matriarch revives once** (Empowered is permanent, §443) unless Tornado Warrior's end-of-turn disempower, Star-Crossed or Emperor's Dais resets her.
- **Mercenary needs two Power spent this turn** for +2 and Ganking. Ezreal's discount can take Accelerate's Chaos away.
- **Defy can't counter Punch First or Switcheroo** (two Power; Defy reaches one).
- **A hidden card at a battlefield you lose is trashed.**

## Mulligans

Keep almost everything (the simulator's best policy is keep all). **Bottom up to two** from a hand with no 2-drop, or with two 4-drops and nothing cheaper. 2-drop in the opening 4: 71%; by turn 2: 85%.
