## Executive summary: the game plan

*Read this before every round.*

**What the deck is.** A midrange deck that **holds one battlefield (call it A) and makes attacking it a mistake.** Cheap bodies hold A; a face-down card waits there; Ambush units and tricks punish whoever walks in. Then it turns the trap around: **Irresistible Faefolk pulls the other battlefield's defender into A, and Sivir walks into the battlefield they just left empty.**

**The closing turn, in one line.** Start your turn on **6 holding A**: the hold scores → **7**. Faefolk walks into A and pulls B's defender in; the fight happens at A (a face-down **Bedeviling Stranger** can bounce the unit you pulled); **Sivir** comes out of the Champion Zone with Accelerate and walks into the empty B → **8**. Holding at 7 also wins, on its own.

**Why it works.** Sivir is never in your hand to be discarded or drawn late, and the closing turn is cheap: Faefolk's walk and a face-down Stranger cost nothing, Sivir with Accelerate costs 5 Energy. Everything else you hold is spare for backup.

**What each part of the deck does.**

| job | cards |
|---|---|
| **Pull them in** | Faefolk ×3 · Evelynn (face down) |
| **Punish them at A** | Bedeviling Stranger ×2 · Switcheroo ×2 · Punch First ×3 · Nidalee ×3 and Rengar ×2 (Ambush) · Vex ×2 |
| **Hold A** | Staunch ×2 and the other two-drops (8 two-drop units in all) |
| **Clear the way** | Rampage ×2 · Rebuke ×2 · Gust ×2 · Star-Crossed ×2 |
| **Close at B** | Sivir · Ride the Wind ×3 · Fizz ×2 |
| **Cards** | The Good Stuff (returns to hand each time A holds) · Ezreal ×2 · Innovator ×3 · Nidalee |

**Four rules for every game.**

1. **From 5 points, check every turn whether either player can reach 8 before the other acts again** ([strategy.md §0](../docs/strategy.md)). A hold at 7 wins and is never gated (§473.1.a.1).
2. **A is the whole game.** Spend cards to keep it; lose it and you lose the face-down card there and The Good Stuff with it (§323.6.a, §830.1.b).
3. **Their Vex, Apathetic dies before Sivir is played.** She stuns any unit you play while she's at a battlefield, wherever you play it, and a stunned Sivir can't walk to B.
4. **Hold Faefolk back.** It's a 1-Might unit whose job is one move on the closing turn. Keep one in base.

**What to bring:** game 1, **Sigil of the Storm** · after you lose a game (you'll choose to go first), **Amateur Recital** · after you win one (they choose, and usually go first), **Forbidding Waste** (§8).

## 3. The game, turn by turn

### Turns 1–2: claim A

- **A two-drop, walked into a battlefield.** Faefolk, Stranger and Evelynn cost no Power; Staunch is the best holder but costs a rune's Power. **On the play you have 2 runes:** Staunch means a rune short on turn 2.
- **Which battlefield is A?** The one you can keep. Prefer the one they'd have to walk the furthest units into, and never Rockfall Path ("units can't be played here": it kills Ambush and Stranger). Forbidding Waste punishes a lone *defender*, so it's a better B than A.
- **Faefolk isn't your holder.** If it's your only two-drop, play it, but its real job is the closing turn. Keep at least one back in base from turn 4 on.

### Turns 3–4: arm the trap, win on interaction

- **Hide a card at A.** Stranger when their attacks will be 2-on-1 or wider; Switcheroo when fights will be 1-on-1 (Stranger needs 4 units, itself included). One face-down card per battlefield you control (§811.1.b), placed on your turn, playable from the **next turn on, theirs included**, at Reaction speed and for no cost.
- **Pay the hide with Gold when you can.** The cost is one Power of any colour: a Gold token pays it, or a recycled rune, and recycling a rune triggers the legend's Gold.
- **Keep Reaction answers up on their turn:** Gust (1 Energy) for their 2–3-Might attacker, Nidalee or Rengar by Ambush, Star-Crossed. This is where the pros said the game is won.
- **Put Vex at a battlefield.** Every unit they play is stunned, wherever it lands, so their Ambush blockers and ready-on-arrival units can't fight that turn.
- **The Good Stuff on a quiet turn.** Deploy it to A when you don't need Energy held up (usually turn 5+). From then on, each time A holds it comes back to your hand at the start of your turn (§383.4.d.2.b), ready to replay for another 3 cards.

### From 5 points: the terminal scan

Every turn, both players: can either reach 8 before the other acts again? **Their win next turn outranks everything.** Then look for yours (§4). At 7 holding A, you have nothing to do but keep A.

## 4. The closing turn, rule by rule

Checked step by step against the rules on 1 November (handbook → verbatim → FAQ → rulings). **You start on 6, holding A, with Faefolk ready in base and ideally Stranger face down at A.**

1. **Beginning Phase:** you hold A → **7** (§315.2.b.2). If The Good Stuff is at A, it returns to your hand now.
2. **Faefolk walks base → A.** You control A, so nothing is contested and no fight starts (§190.3.a.1). Its trigger moves **B's defender** to A. The pull is a target, so Baron Nashor can't be pulled (§355.7, §757).
3. **A fight starts at A at once.** Their pulled unit is the **attacker** (it arrived where they don't control), you're the defender (§466.2.c). There's no open window first: the cleanup stages a combat showdown and **they get focus first** (§323.12, §466.2.c.1.a).
4. **When focus reaches you, flip Stranger** (free from face down, §811.1.b; it goes to A, §811.1.d.1). Your holder + Faefolk + their unit + Stranger = 4, counted when the effect resolves (§383.4.a.2), so you may return their unit. **Don't pass while you hold focus**: if both players pass in a row, damage happens.
5. **No attacker, no damage.** You win the combat and keep A (§467.1, §468.5).
6. **B is empty.** It went uncontrolled in the cleanup after the pull (§323.5), and **any face-down card they had at B was trashed** (§323.6.a).
7. **Sivir:** play her from the Champion Zone with Accelerate: she enters ready at your base (§355.2.a, §805.6), 2 Power spent, 6 Might. **Walk base → B** (no Ganking needed, §144.4.a). You can only move once A's combat is over (§144.1.c).
8. **B's showdown** (you get focus first) closes with only your unit there: **conquer → 8** (§348.2.a.1). You scored A this turn by holding it, so the 8th point is open (§473.1.b.1).

**Stranger is insurance, not the line.** If you have no Stranger, the fight at A happens. If **any defender of yours survives**, their attacker goes to *their base*, not back to B (§468.1.a.2), so B is still empty and Sivir still walks in. Punch First or a face-down Switcheroo wins that fight. The only real danger is their pulled unit winning A: then they conquer A on your turn and score a point.

**At 6, holding nothing:** attack A with your board and move Faefolk in with the attack: its pull brings B's defender into A, and you stay the attacker there (§190.3.a.1, §466.2.c.1). Win the fight, conquer A → 7; then Sivir walks into the empty B → 8 (both battlefields scored this turn).

**At 5 holding A:** hold → 6, conquer the empty B → 7, and the next start-of-turn hold wins.

**Other second conquests** if Sivir is answered: Rengar by Ambush (even where only enemy units are), Ride the Wind (moves and readies, even in a showdown), Fizz recasting Ride the Wind.

**What breaks it** (have an answer before you start):
- **Their Vex, Apathetic** at any battlefield: Sivir and any Faefolk played that turn are stunned and can't move. **Rebuke her first** (her Deflect makes it 3 Power, which turns on Sivir's condition by itself).
- **Responses to Stranger:** a Gust or Star-Crossed on Stranger makes the bounce miss (§811.1.d.3.a); killing one of your units drops the count to 3; Not So Fast counters the bounce *and* Faefolk's pull.
- **Answers to Sivir in B's showdown:** Rebuke, Hidden Blade, Moonfall, Star-Crossed, Siren's Song. Gust can't (she's 6).
- **Baron Nashor** can't be pulled or bounced, and **the Baron Pit** is a third battlefield: a conquest for the 8th point would need it scored too (this repo's reading, not a staff ruling). **Against Baron, win by holding at 7.**

**Sequencing against Defy** (counters a spell of 4 Energy or less and 1 Power or less, printed cost, §206): **safe:** Punch First, Rebuke, Switcheroo, every unit; **counterable:** Ride the Wind, Gust, Rampage, Star-Crossed. Against Calm, close with units and the 2-Power spells.

**Ezreal's discount on Sivir:** take the **Energy** off her Accelerate, not the rune. A Power cost reduced to 0 isn't spent (§445.1.b), and she'd lose her +2 and Ganking.

## 5. The trap on their turn

They move into A. **Their units applied Contested, so they attack and get focus first** (§466.2.c.1.a). Your answers, in the order they're best:

| their attack | your answer | why |
|---|---|---|
| **2+ units, or 2-on-1 against your holders** | flip **Stranger**, return the biggest attacker | 4+ units counting Stranger; no Might limit; free |
| **1-on-1** | flip **Switcheroo** (swap their big Might with your small one), or **Gust** a 3-Might attacker | Stranger only makes 3 units here and does nothing |
| **one big attacker** | **Nidalee or Rengar by Ambush** into A, then Punch First if needed | Ambush plays them as a Reaction to a battlefield where you have units (§822) |
| **they flood A** | Graves from the sideboard | he deals his Might to every enemy there when he defends; no readying needed |

- **If no defender of yours survives, they conquer A**: your face-down card goes to the trash (§323.6.a) and The Good Stuff dies at their next hold (a conquest alone doesn't kill it, §830.1.b).
- **Kills ready Sivir** if she's on the board ("When one or more enemy units die, ready me").
- **The trap works by doubt.** A face-down card at A could be Stranger, Switcheroo or Evelynn; the top Chaos decks all play the same package, so opponents know the threat but not the card. One reviewer called Stranger the best card in Radiance for exactly this ("how on earth is the opponent supposed to figure out how to attack?", Ultimate Guard, 20 October).

**The counter-plan is to ignore A.** Control decks hold B and build. That's what Faefolk and Evelynn are for: **the trap comes to them.** Pull their B unit into A on your turn, fight it where your tricks are, and Sivir takes B.

## 6. Card by card

- **Sivir, Mercenary.** Always available. Her +2 and Ganking need 2 Power spent *this turn*: Accelerate does it on her own turn; otherwise Punch First, Rebuke or Switcheroo **from hand**. A card played from face down ignores its whole cost (§811.1.b), so a hidden Switcheroo spends no Power.
- **Irresistible Faefolk.** Any move triggers it, including a walk from base. The pull is a target (Baron can't be chosen) and can come from their base too. Never your only holder late.
- **Bedeviling Stranger.** From face down it's played to its own battlefield and its choices are pinned there (§811.1.d.1, §811.1.d.3). It counts itself. From hand (2 Energy, your main phase) it can choose any battlefield: against wide decks, bounce one of four-plus units at their battlefield. **Judge question, open:** whether a face-down Stranger may choose its own battlefield as "an option at that battlefield" (§811.1.d.3) has no ruling. It's clearly the card's intent; ask a judge before an event.
- **Evelynn, Entrancing.** Hide her at A; on your turn she pulls an enemy unit from a different location to A, where it attacks you. **She and Stranger can't both be face down at A.** She's the backup puller.
- **Staunch Survivor.** 4 Might whenever it fights: the holder that wins 1-on-1s.
- **Reckless Innovator.** Accelerate, then a Gold or a paid draw every time it exhausts. Gold pays your hides.
- **Nidalee, Cat Form.** The Ambush defender: a 4-Might Reaction into A, and a card when she wins.
- **Ezreal, Prodigy.** Card flow on a body; his discount covers Accelerate (take the Energy on Sivir) and Rampage's +2.
- **Fizz, Trickster.** Recasts Ride the Wind, Gust, Rampage, Rebuke or Star-Crossed from the trash for its Power alone.
- **Vex, Apathetic.** Stuns **every** unit they play while she's at a battlefield, anywhere. Their Ambush and ready-on-arrival blockers can't fight that turn.
- **Rengar, Trophy Hunter.** 6 Might by Ambush; can also land where only enemy units are, so he's a second conquest at B.
- **Punch First.** +5 for 1 Energy, Defy-proof, and 2 Power: turns on Sivir by itself. The deck's best card.
- **Gust.** 1 Energy at Reaction speed: their 2- and 3-Might units, or their Stranger before it bounces.
- **Ride the Wind.** Move and ready, battlefield to battlefield, even in a showdown (§451.1).
- **Rebuke.** Defy-proof bounce, any size. **Your answer to their Vex.**
- **Switcheroo.** Wins a fight; doesn't stop a bounce or a kill. From face down at A, both targets must be at A (§811.1.d.2).
- **Rampage.** The only kill. Clean against decks without Defy (Rengar, Kennen, Evelynn); weak into Calm.
- **Star-Crossed.** Bounces one of theirs and one of yours: clears a blocker, or saves Sivir at the cost of her attack.
- **The Good Stuff.** Draw 3 (discard 1) at A; back to hand every time A holds. Dies only when *they* hold A. Expect An Offer You Can't Refuse, Detonate and Acceptable Losses against it.

## 7. Power, Energy and the odds

- **Energy is the bottleneck.** Hold up Gust and Ambush on their turn; play The Good Stuff on turns you don't need to.
- **Power is cheap here.** The legend makes a Gold whenever you recycle a rune (if she's ready), and Gold pays Power of any colour, including the cost of hiding (riftbound.gg's Radiance write-up makes the same point about this legend).
- **A rune can do both in one turn:** exhaust for Energy, then recycle for Power (`docs/rules.md`, the rune formula).
- **Runes 6/6.** Power demand is Chaos 19, Body 13; a 5/7 split moves turn-3 colour misses only from 7% to 6% on the play, under the 3-point bar for changing.

**How often you'll have a piece** (39-card library; about 11 cards seen by the closing turn):

| piece | copies | by 7 cards | by 11 cards |
|---|---|---|---|
| any two-drop unit | 8 | 83% | 95% |
| a puller (Faefolk or Evelynn) | 4 | 56% | 75% |
| Faefolk | 3 | 46% | 64% |
| Stranger or Switcheroo | 4 | 56% | 75% |
| Stranger | 2 | 33% | 49% |
| The Good Stuff | 1 | 18% | 28% |

Stranger is there about half the time; the backup (win the fight at A) comes up a lot.

## 8. Battlefields, mulligans, sideboarding

**Battlefields.** In a best-of-3 each player picks one of their three, placed at once; one used in a decided game is retired (§488.5). Random at a single-game event (§487.5). **Battlefields are revealed before turn order** (Core Rules §113 before §115): game 1 is blind, and in games 2–3 the loser of the previous game chooses the seat *after* seeing both (TR §407.4). So pick by the last result, not by the seat:

| when | battlefield | why |
|---|---|---|
| **Game 1 (blind)** | **Sigil of the Storm**: "When you conquer here, you must recycle one of your runes." | it doesn't reward holding, so it can't feed whoever sits on it. For you a recycle can make a Gold; for them a lost rune per conquest, which slows Baron and Astral Heron |
| **After you lose a game** (you choose, so go first) | **Amateur Recital**: "When you hold here, you may move a unit at a battlefield to its base." | **the trap's engine**, below |
| **After you win a game** (they choose, and going first wins more, so expect the draw) | **Forbidding Waste**: "While a unit here is defending alone, it has −2 Might." | no hold reward to hand them; it punishes their lone early holder. Hold it with two and the −2 never touches you |

**Amateur Recital, the engine.** Going first, your turn-2 move reaches a battlefield before theirs (unless they Accelerate a unit on turn 1: going second they have 3 runes). Make Recital your A. From your turn-3 hold, every turn:
- the hold scores, and its trigger (your Beginning Phase, before you draw) **sends their lone defender at B to its base** (§383.4.d.2.b);
- B goes uncontrolled once the trigger resolves and **any face-down card they had there is trashed** (§323.5, §323.6.a);
- in your main phase, walk a ready unit into B and conquer it. **Two points a turn.** From 6 it wins: hold → 7, conquer B → 8 (§473.1.b.1; Riot's FAQ gives this exact line).
- **Two defenders at B:** Recital sends one home, Faefolk pulls the other into the trap.
- **It chooses**, so Baron and "can't be chosen" units are immune, Deflect taxes it, and they can respond: a Reaction unit or a face-down card played at B keeps it occupied.
- **It's symmetric.** If they hold Recital, *their* hold sends your lone holder home and trashes your face-down card. Hold it with two, keep a face-down card there to flip in response, and **if they ever take it, retaking it comes first.**
- **Evidence:** none for it. 15 vouched lists since Radiance, no top-quarter finish; no guide recommends it; Ultimate Guard's go-first example is Targon's Peak, the safe alternative. It's here because a deck built to keep one battlefield is the deck its downside doesn't hurt. Log whether you claimed it and how often it fired.

**Mulligans** (set aside up to 2; they go to the bottom):
- **Want two two-drops and a 3-drop.** Keep Stranger, Faefolk, Staunch, Evelynn, Switcheroo, Nidalee.
- **Send back first:** Rengar, Rampage, Star-Crossed and The Good Stuff in a hand with no two-drop.
- **Turn-1 two-drop:** 8 in the library, so 70% keeping your first 5 cards and 83% when you mulligan for one.

**Sideboarding** (untested; from the card reads in §9):

| in | out | against | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Repulse** | Rampage or The Good Stuff | **Diana, LeBlanc, Riven, Kennen, Mordekaiser, Irelia, Evelynn** | counters a spell aimed at one of your units: Sivir in B, or your holder |
| **Graves ×2** | Rebuke, Ride the Wind | **Rengar, Seraphine, wide decks** | defends A against a flood: his Might to every enemy there |
| **Sabotage** | Rampage | **Vex Gloomist, Kai'Sa, Seraphine** | takes Back Off, Switcheroo or Time Warp out of their hand before they hide or cast it |
| **Unyielding Spirit ×2** | Stranger, The Good Stuff | **Ziggs, Kai'Sa, Akali** | prevents all spell and ability damage this turn (Bombs, pings, Frozen Fortress) |
| **Ruin Runner** | Rampage | **Diana, Riven, Akali** | a holder they can't Gust, Moonfall, Star-Crossed or bounce |
| **Ravenbloom Prefect** | Rampage | **Orianna, Ziggs, Jayce** | banishes a gear they play |
| **Gust #3, Star-Crossed #3** | Rampage, The Good Stuff | **Diana, Irelia** | more cheap Reactions against many small bodies |

**Rampage comes out against Calm** (Riven, Vex, Akali, Irelia, K'Sante, Yi run Defy in most lists) and against decks whose units want to die (LeBlanc, Mordekaiser).

### The local room in detail (Sip & Play)

**Rengar** (63 lists, 31 vouched; the widest deck here). **Best matchup.**
- He attacks, so A fills up; Stranger fires. He runs no Gust, Star-Crossed, Defy or Not So Fast in 10%+ of lists.
- **Watch:** Thrill of the Hunt (94%) saves a bounced unit or drops one into B against Sivir; **his own Faefolk (90%) pulls your holders out of A**, so keep two there; Brynhir (22%) stops you playing cards that turn.
- Your Vex at a battlefield stuns every Ambush unit he plays. Rampage is clean (no Calm). Graves in.

**LeBlanc** (57 / 20). **Good.**
- Wide and attacks; bounce is what she fears. **But** Deathgrip and Sacrifice (89% each) kill her own target in response: the bounce misses and she gets her death triggers. Bait them before the Stranger turn.
- Hidden Blade (84%) kills Sivir in B or a holder: Repulse in. Rampage out (deaths feed her).

**Kai'Sa** (93 / 37). **Good for the closing turn.**
- She doesn't walk into A; she kills your holders from range (Hextech Ray, Falling Star, Bellows Breath), and **Frozen Fortress** (89%) deals 1 to each unit there each Beginning Phase, killing a Faefolk.
- **Nothing she plays at Action speed kills a 6-Might Sivir** (Hextech Ray is 3, or 4 with Void Gate), and she has no Gust, Star-Crossed or Vex in 10%+ of lists. Keep Faefolk in base; **never make Rockfall Path (90%) your A**; Brynhir (95%) turns the trap off for a turn.
- Unyielding Spirit ×2 and Sabotage in.

**Vex, Gloomist** (79 / 22). **Worst-two.**
- She holds and builds; she won't walk into A. **Her Vex, Apathetic (96%) stuns Sivir the turn you play her.** Back Off (91%), Defy, Discipline, her own Switcheroo and Stranger.
- **Plan:** Rebuke her Vex first (3 Power with Deflect, so it also turns Sivir on); or play Sivir a turn early. This is a race to hold at 7, not a trap game. Sabotage and Repulse in; Rampage out.

**Diana** (41 / 12). **Worst.**
- Her Energy works only in showdowns, so she answers in every fight: **Gust (100%) on Stranger, Moonfall (100%) pulls Sivir out of B**, Vex (83%), Hard Bargain (76%) counters Switcheroo, Star-Crossed, Eclipse, Smoke Screen.
- **Plan:** make her spend first; Gust her 2–3-Might bodies; Repulse for Sivir; Ruin Runner as a holder she can't touch.

### Cards in the field that answer the trap

**No card in the legal pool removes face-down cards; you lose one only by losing A.** These are the ones to know (% of that legend's lists):

- **Scuttle Crab** (when it dies, they see your face-down cards that turn): Irelia 84%, Yi 74%, Riven 66%, Akali 53%.
- **Their Vex, Apathetic** (stuns what you play): Vex 96%, Diana 83%, Irelia 61%, Evelynn 59%.
- **Not So Fast** (counters Stranger's bounce and Faefolk's pull): Akali 76%, Irelia 76%, Riven 43%.
- **Charm, Blitzcrank, their own Faefolk, Moonfall** (move your holder or Sivir): Yi 90%, Rengar 90%, Diana 100%, Riven 71%.
- **Rockfall Path** ("units can't be played here"): Kai'Sa 90%. Don't make it your A.
- **An Offer You Can't Refuse, Detonate, Acceptable Losses** (gear removal, for The Good Stuff): Kennen 54%, Ziggs 89%.

## 11. Mistakes that lose games

- **Playing Sivir into their Vex, Apathetic.** Stunned, she can't walk to B. Rebuke Vex first.
- **Passing with focus in the fight at A** before you've flipped Stranger.
- **Making Faefolk your only holder**, or playing every Faefolk early. Keep one in base.
- **Hiding Stranger for a 1-on-1.** It needs 4 units; hide Switcheroo there.
- **Losing A cheaply.** It takes your face-down card and The Good Stuff with it.
- **Choosing Rockfall Path as A.**
- **Trying to conquer for the 8th point with a Baron Pit out.** Hold at 7 instead.
- **Taking the rune off Sivir's Accelerate with Ezreal.** Take the Energy.
- **Playing The Good Stuff on a turn you need Energy up** for their attack.

## What this guide does not claim

- **No win rates and no precedent.** None of the 5 Battle Mistress lists posted since 23 October runs Stranger or Faefolk, and none has a vouched finish. This plan is untested by anyone.
- **The test bench can't see the deck's main tricks** (the pull, the bounce, The Good Stuff's draw).

- **Sideboard plans are from card reads, not games.**
