## Executive summary: the game plan

*Read this before every round.*

**What the deck is.** A raid deck. Go in, conquer, score, and don't build around holding a battlefield: the field (Kai'Sa, Diana, Vex, Riven) is built to remove whatever sits on one. **The plan is to reach 6 points, then conquer both battlefields in one turn** (the first conquest makes 7, the second 8).

**The finisher you never have to draw.** Sivir starts in the Champion Zone. Played with Accelerate (1 Energy and 1 Chaos more), she enters **ready**, and her own Power plus the Accelerate's Chaos is the 2 Power her text asks for: **6 Might and Ganking** the turn she lands, with no other card.

**The finishing turn, in one line.** Your board attacks battlefield A; **Irresistible Faefolk** moves in with them and pulls B's defender into A; Sivir comes in ready and walks into the empty B. Two conquests, 6 to 8. (**Evelynn** does the same pull only from a battlefield you already control: the holding line in §4.)

**What each part of the deck does.**

| job | cards |
|---|---|
| **The core** (never cut without game evidence) | Faefolk ×3 · Evelynn · Reckless Innovator ×3 · Fizz ×2 · Nilah ×2 · Rengar ×2 · Punch First ×3 · Ride the Wind ×3 · Sivir |
| **Threats that act the turn they land** | Sivir, Nilah (Accelerate), Rengar, Nidalee (Ambush), Innovator (Accelerate) |
| **Open a battlefield** | Faefolk, Evelynn (pull; Evelynn only from a battlefield you control) · Rebuke, Star-Crossed, Gust (bounce) · Rampage (kill) · Vex (stun what they play) |
| **The extra action on the finishing turn** | Ride the Wind, First Mate, Portable Portal, Punch First, Fizz (recasts a spell) |
| **Protect the finishing turn** | Sabotage (before you commit) · Switcheroo ×2 (win the fight) · Star-Crossed (save Sivir, ending her attack) |
| **Cards and Gold** | Ezreal ×2, Innovator, Nidalee; the legend turns recycled runes into Gold |

**Three rules for every game.**

1. **From 5 points, check every turn whether either player can reach 8 before the other acts again** ([strategy.md §0](../docs/strategy.md)). A hold at 7 wins too.
2. **Don't spend the finishing turn to get to 6.** Keep Sivir's Accelerate Energy, a pull and one protection card for the last turn.
3. **Two threats, never one.** Every good opponent has one answer to a single attacker. The pull plus Sivir, or Sivir plus Rengar, beats one answer.

**What to bring:** Sigil of the Storm when the pick is blind · Targon's Peak going first · Forbidding Waste on the draw (§7).

## 2. How it got here

- **29 October:** after an 0–4 Nexus Night, the old Sivir was rebuilt around one idea: Sivir is the finisher, and every expensive threat must act the turn it lands (Graves, Beast Below and Zed all died before acting). Plan agreed: raid, race to 6, win in one turn.
- **30–31 October:** Jay rebuilt the draft himself around **displacement**: Faefolk ×3, Nilah, Portable Portal, three Ride the Wind.
- **31 October, the audit:** three independent reviews (data, play, rules) and a second reviewer's proposals. Every change was made like-for-like by job: The Good Stuff and a Portal became Ezreal ×2 (draw on a body); Abandon became Switcheroo and then Sabotage (protection that doesn't need 2 Energy held up); Sabotage became Rampage #2 (a kill opens a battlefield this turn); two Bedeviling Strangers became Switcheroo and Evelynn; one Vex became a second Switcheroo; one Star-Crossed became Gust (Energy). The battlefields were re-picked from all 71.

The full reasoning and the numbers are in the worksheet's §5.

## 3. The game, turn by turn

### Turns 1–3: develop, score early

- **Turn 1:** a 2-drop. Staunch is the best early scorer (4 Might attacking). Faefolk can wait in base; its job comes later. **On the play you have 2 runes:** Staunch's Power means exhausting both and recycling one, which leaves you a rune short on turn 2. Faefolk and Evelynn cost no Power.
- **Turns 2–3:** a 3-drop. Innovator or Nilah with Accelerate attacks the turn it lands. Ezreal smooths a weak hand.
- **What to expect:** on the play, `scripts/sim` averages 0.5 units on the board by the end of turn 1, so often there won't be one. It catches up: two or more units by turn 3 in 98% of its games.

### Turns 3–5: race to 6

- **Raid, don't hold.** Take battlefields, score, and accept that they'll take them back. A conquered battlefield has already scored.
- **Take free holds**, but don't spend cards defending one.
- **Kill when it readies the legend.** "When one or more enemy units die, ready me": a fight that kills readies Battle Mistress for another Gold.
- **Hide Evelynn** at a battlefield you control when you can. Her pull works from there, so it's for the holding line in §4; if they retake that battlefield, she's lost (§323.6.a).

### From 5 points: the terminal scan

Every turn, both players: can either reach 8 before the other acts again? **Their win next turn outranks everything else.** Then look for yours (§4).

## 4. The finishing turn

**At 6 points, holding nothing:**

1. **Attack A** with your board. Move Faefolk in with them; its trigger pulls B's defender into A. You stay the attacker at A (§190.3.a.1, §466.2.c.1). The pull resolves before the fight starts, and they can only answer it with Reactions.
2. **The fight at A** resolves; conquer: **7**.
3. **Sivir.** Play her with Accelerate. Units enter at your base or a battlefield you control (§355.2.a), exhausted unless Accelerate applies (§143.4); hers does. She has spent 2 Power: **6 Might, Ganking**. Move base → B (no Ganking needed, §144.4). B is empty, so the showdown closes with you in control: conquer, **8** (§348.2.a.1).

**At 6, holding A going in:** the hold scores at the start of your turn: **7**. Then conquer B for **8**. At 7 the 8th point needs every battlefield scored this turn (§473.1.b.1), and A just was.

**Evelynn's line** (she's hidden at A, which you hold): after the hold makes 7, play her from face down and pull B's defender into A. That unit arrives at a battlefield its controller doesn't control, so **they become the attacker at A** (§190.3.a.1) and the fight there happens first. B is empty: Sivir walks in for 8. Check first that a conquest at A can't win the game for *them*.

**At 7 with a hold:** the hold wins (§473.1.a.1).

**Other ways to make the second conquest** if Sivir is answered or the line is blocked:
- **Nilah:** Accelerate and Ganking from base.
- **Rengar:** Ambush, even onto a battlefield with only enemy units.
- **Ride the Wind:** moves and readies any unit, even mid-showdown.
- **First Mate:** readies a unit.
- **Portable Portal:** readies a unit and gives your units there Ganking.

**Two defenders at B:** pull one into A, and Sivir's 6 (11 with Punch First) takes the one left. On Forbidding Waste, the one left defends at −2.

**Baron Nashor can't be chosen by your spells and abilities**, so nothing in the deck bounces or pulls him. Against Baron, see §9.

**Sequencing against Defy** (it counters a spell of 4 Energy or less and 1 Power or less, judged on the printed cost, §206):
- **Defy can't touch:** Punch First, Rebuke, Switcheroo (2 Power each), and every unit.
- **Defy can counter:** Ride the Wind, Gust, Sabotage, Rampage (Fizz's recast too), Star-Crossed.
- So against Calm, make the finishing turn out of units and the 2-Power spells, or cast Sabotage first to draw out the Defy.

**Ezreal's discount on Sivir:** take the **Energy** off her Accelerate, not the rune. A Power cost reduced to 0 isn't spent (§445.1.b), and she'd lose her +2 and Ganking.

**Don't pull onto your own battlefield.** If Faefolk pulls an enemy unit onto a battlefield you control, they become the attacker there (§190.3.a.1) and can conquer it on your turn.

## 5. Card by card

- **Sivir, Mercenary.** Always available, so hands without a finisher are fine. Her +2 and Ganking switch on as soon as you've spent 2 Power this turn: her own cost plus Accelerate, Punch First, Rebuke, or Switcheroo **cast from hand**. A card played from face down ignores its whole cost (§811.1.b), so a hidden Switcheroo spends no Power.
- **Irresistible Faefolk.** The deck's namesake. Any move triggers it, including an ordinary move from base, so it can sit in base until it's needed. A 1-Might body; its value is the pull.
- **Evelynn, Entrancing.** Hide her (one rune of any colour) at a battlefield you control; from your next turn she can be played from there ignoring her cost, and on your turn she pulls an enemy unit from elsewhere to her battlefield. **That battlefield is always yours, so the pulled unit becomes the attacker there**: her pull empties the *other* battlefield, it doesn't help a fight. If they retake her battlefield, she's lost (§323.6.a). Worst case she's a 2-Energy, 2-Might body.
- **Staunch Survivor.** 4 Might whenever it fights. The turn-1 play, with the Power caveat in §3.
- **Reckless Innovator.** Accelerate, then a Gold or a card each time it exhausts (attacking exhausts it). An engine on a body.
- **Ezreal, Prodigy.** Draws on arrival. His discount covers Nilah's and Innovator's Accelerate and Rampage's +2. Not Portable Portal's payment (that's a triggered ability's cost).
- **Fizz, Trickster.** Replays Ride the Wind, Gust, Rampage, Rebuke or Star-Crossed from the trash for its Power alone. Rampage and Gust cost nothing; the spell is recycled afterwards.
- **Nilah.** A 4-Might Ganker that can act the turn it lands. Your second finisher.
- **Nidalee.** Ambush (played as a Reaction to a battlefield where you have units, §822) adds a second threat mid-fight; draws on a win.
- **First Mate.** Readies a unit; on the finishing turn, a unit that already attacked.
- **Vex, Apathetic.** Not a raid threat: she earns her slot by stunning blockers they play at her battlefield (Ambush and ready-on-arrival units deal no combat damage that turn). Deflect taxes their answers to her.
- **Rengar, Trophy Hunter.** 6 Might from Ambush; can land on a battlefield with only enemy units on it.
- **Punch First.** +5 Might; 2 Power, so Defy can't stop it, and it meets Sivir's condition on its own.
- **Ride the Wind.** Move and ready, battlefield to battlefield, at Action speed. The most flexible second action. Defy-able.
- **Rebuke.** Bounces any unit at a battlefield; Defy-proof. **Your answer to an opposing Vex, Apathetic:** with her Deflect it costs 3 Power, which meets Sivir's condition by itself.
- **Star-Crossed.** Bounces one of theirs and one of yours. It clears a blocker, or saves Sivir from removal at the cost of her attack.
- **Rampage.** The deck's only kill. Your unit and theirs deal their Mights to each other; with Ezreal out, the +2 is free.
- **Switcheroo.** Swaps two units' Mights at one battlefield. **It wins a fight** (undoes a −4 shrink, or trades Sivir's 6 against a small blocker); it does **not** stop a bounce or a kill. Hidden, so it can sit face down; played from there it costs nothing and so spends no Power toward Sivir. Defy can't counter it either way (it reads the printed cost, §206).
- **Sabotage.** Your-turn only. Cast it **before** committing Sivir: if they Defy it, their Defy is gone for 1 Energy and 1 Power; if it resolves, take the card that wrecks your turn (Defy, Smoke Screen, Eclipse, a Zhonya's still in hand). It can't take units or cards already hidden on the board.
- **Gust.** A 1-Energy bounce for a unit of 3 Might or less, at Reaction speed; free from Fizz.
- **Portable Portal.** Deploy can go to any battlefield (§830.1.b.1); readies a unit there for 1 Body and gives your units there Ganking. It needs a unit of yours already there to be worth it.

### Interactions worth knowing

- **Faefolk + Forbidding Waste:** pull one of two defenders away; the other is alone and at −2.
- **Fizz + Rampage or Gust:** a free recast. **Fizz + Punch First** replays the +5 for 2 Power.
- **Ezreal + Accelerate units:** each of Nilah, Innovator and Sivir costs 1 less to Accelerate (take the Energy on Sivir).
- **Kills ready the legend:** Rampage, a won fight, or Rengar trading up readies Battle Mistress for another Gold.

## 6. Power and Energy

- **Energy is the bottleneck; Power is plentiful.** The legend makes a Gold whenever you recycle a rune (if she's ready), and Gold pays Power of either colour. So cards cheap in Energy and paid in Power are this deck's best cards.
- **A rune can do both in one turn:** exhaust it for Energy, then recycle it for Power (`docs/rules.md`, the rune formula: a card costing E Energy and P Power needs max(E, P) runes, E of them ready).
- **The numbers** (the test bench, normal pressure, against Jay's list on the morning of 31 October): Sivir's condition is on in 48% of turns 4–8 (was 41%); tricks crowded out by Energy 1.55 a game (1.44). The 6/6 rune split is still the best (every split from 3/9 to 9/3 lands within 0.3 Energy).

## 7. Battlefields and mulligans

**Battlefields.** In a best-of-3, each player picks one of their three, placed at the same time; one used in a decided game is retired for the match (§488.5). At a single-game event it's random (§487.5). Every battlefield works for both players.

| when | battlefield | why |
|---|---|---|
| **Blind** | **Sigil of the Storm**: "When you conquer here, you must recycle one of your runes." (errata) | For you, that recycle can make a Gold (if the legend is ready). For them it's just a lost rune, which slows Baron Nashor and Astral Heron. The most-played Sivir battlefield over the archive (22 of 38 Battle Mistress lists), but almost all of those are from before Radiance: none of the 5 posted since 23 October runs it. Cost: you lose a rune too |
| **Going first** | **Targon's Peak**: "When you conquer here, ready up to 2 runes at the end of this turn." (errata) | You usually conquer it first. Two ready runes on their turn pay for Gust, or Rebuke in a showdown (it's an Action); not Star-Crossed (3 Energy). If they conquer it, they get the same on your turn. It doesn't speed their Baron or Heron (everyone's runes ready on their own turn) |
| **On the draw** | **Forbidding Waste**: "While a unit here is defending alone, it has −2 Might." | They move in first and usually hold with one unit; this pays the player hitting back, and you rarely defend alone |

At events testing Riot's new setup (turn order decided before battlefields), there's no blind pick: choose by seat.

**Mulligans** (set aside up to 2, draw that many, the set-aside cards go to the bottom, §117 via `docs/rules.md`):
- **Keep most hands.** The goldfish's best policy is to keep everything; Sivir is always available, so a hand with no finisher is fine.
- **Want:** a 2-drop and a 3-drop. **Keep first:** Vex, Innovator, Rengar, Faefolk, Evelynn.
- **Send back first:** Sabotage, Punch First, Switcheroo (finishing-turn cards that do nothing early).
- **Turn-1 odds:** with 6 two-drop units in the 39-card library, you see one by turn 1 in 59% of games keeping your first 5 cards, and 72% when you mulligan for one (about 7 cards seen).

## 9. What changes against each kind of opponent

Archetypes as `docs/archetypes.md` names them. Card reads from the matchups worksheet. **None of this is tested.**

### Aggro (Kha'Zix, Rek'Sai, Darius, Jarvan, Lucian, Ornn, Ambessa)
- **They race you.** Don't fall behind on board early: Staunch and the Accelerate units are your defence too.
- **Kill when it readies the legend**; small bodies die to Rampage and fights.
- **Two units at every fight** against Kha'Zix (he wants your units alone).
- Graves comes in against go-wide boards.

### Tempo (Irelia, Evelynn, Diana, Azir, Lillia, Ziggs, Pyke)
- **Answers at Reaction speed are the danger.** Diana's Energy works only in showdowns, so she answers during your fights (Eclipse and Smoke Screen −4, Flash, Switcheroo, Star-Crossed, her own Vex). Evelynn flips Hidden cards at Reaction speed.
- **Switcheroo back** a −4 shrink; **Repulse in** against Diana.
- **Raiding suits you against Evelynn:** taking her battlefield removes her hidden cards when control changes (§323.6.a).
- Irelia has the densest tricks and Defy: close when she's tapped out, with Defy-proof cards.

### Midrange (Riven, Kennen, Rengar, LeBlanc, Mordekaiser, Orianna)
- **Riven** (the most-played legend since Radiance, 8% of lists): Calm, Astral Heron, Baron Nashor. **Sigil of the Storm** slows her runes. Her units enter ready at battlefields she controls, which Vex punishes. **Against Baron, win by holding:** the Baron Pit makes a third battlefield, so two conquests don't take 6 to 8 (the repo's own Baron Pit entry, not a staff ruling; ask a judge at an event).
- **Rengar:** Ambush blockers when you attack into open runes; his own Faefolk pulls your units. Close when he's tapped out.
- **Kennen:** Switcheroo and Star-Crossed answer one attacker. Attack with two so one answer can't stop both.
- **LeBlanc:** her units want to die. Bounce, don't kill, and race her engine.
- **Orianna:** her threats are gear turned into units. Ravenbloom Prefect in.

### Control (Kai'Sa, Vex Gloomist, Master Yi Wuju Bladesman, Akali, Viktor, Seraphine)
- **Kai'Sa:** the most removal in the field (Hextech Ray, Falling Star, Smoke Screen, Thousand-Tailed Watcher). No Defy. **Do the finishing turn in one go**, with the first attack drawing her answer. **Brynhir** lands on her turn and stops you playing cards for the rest of it, so don't count on a Reaction (Gust, Star-Crossed) that turn. **Sabotage first:** her answers are spells in hand. Repulse (for Gust) and Star-Crossed #3 (for Vex) in.
- **Vex (Gloomist):** every answer at once: Defy, Discipline, a hidden Back Off, her own Vex, Star-Crossed, Rebuke, Switcheroo. **Rebuke her Vex before you play Sivir.** Sabotage only when she's tapped out; Repulse in for it.
- **Master Yi (Wuju Bladesman):** +2 to a unit defending alone; Defy; Rengar and Ruin Runner. Attack in pairs; Forbidding Waste's −2 cancels his +2 there.
- **Viktor:** a board of Recruits. Graves in.

### Big threats, in any archetype (Astral Heron, Baron Nashor, Rhasa)
- **Race them.** Sigil of the Storm costs them a rune whenever they conquer it.
- A lone 7-Might Heron defends at 5 on Forbidding Waste; Sivir with Punch First is 11.
- **Heron and Rhasa:** Rebuke and Star-Crossed bounce them whatever their size; Rampage usually can't kill them.
- **Baron Nashor can't be chosen by your spells and abilities:** nothing in the deck bounces, pulls or kills him. Win by holding (and see Riven above on the Baron Pit).

## 11. Mistakes that lose games

- **Committing Sivir into open Calm runes** without drawing out the Defy first (Sabotage, or a Defy-proof line).
- **Spending the finishing pieces to reach 6.** Arrive at 6 with Sivir's Energy, a pull and a protection card.
- **One attacker.** Every S-tier deck has one answer for one threat.
- **Pulling an enemy onto your own battlefield.** They become the attacker.
- **Taking the rune off Sivir's Accelerate with Ezreal.** Take the Energy.
- **Holding a battlefield on purpose.** Defending costs cards; raiding scores.
- **Playing Staunch on turn 1 on the play** when Faefolk or Evelynn would do, and then being a rune short.

## 12. What to log

Each game, which of the four it was, so one problem isn't fixed with another's cards:
1. couldn't reach 6
2. reached 6 but couldn't open a battlefield
3. opened one but didn't make the second conquest
