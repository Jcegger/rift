## The short version

*Read before every round.*

**What the deck does: it outlasts them.** Every card you trade or discard comes back. Merchant, Ezreal and Disciple fill the trash with **choices**: a spell for Fizz, a unit for Matriarch. **Fizz** recasts a spell from the trash. **Matriarch** plays a unit from the trash for free. **Tornado** switches Matriarch on without paying. In a long game you still have bodies and tricks when they've run out. That's the midrange: attack slower decks, hold against faster ones, and win the attrition either way.

**The legend's part: renewable Power.** Fizz's recasts, Matriarch's Empower, Punch First, Switcheroo, Star-Crossed, Ezreal and Mercenary all cost Power. Battle Mistress makes it renewable. **Energy is what runs out**, so spend Gold freely on the Power side.

**Your turn, in order:** lethal check, both players → count Gold and runes → **pump before you move** (Punch First on Graves before he goes in) → take open battlefields → develop (Fizz and Matriarch when the trash has targets) → hide (Switcheroo first when a big attacker is coming; Tornado where Matriarch will be) → fight.

**The four rules that matter most:**
1. **Pay Power with Gold first; recycle a rune only for the rest.** The legend starts every turn ready, so your first recycle makes a Gold. A second recycle makes nothing, unless an enemy unit died in between: **recycle, kill, recycle.**
2. **Discard on purpose.** Merchant and Ezreal make you discard. Pitch a spell of 3 Energy or less if Fizz is coming, or a unit of 3 Energy and 1 Power or less if Matriarch is.
3. **The eighth point by Conquer needs both battlefields scored this turn** (§473.1.b). Hold one at the start of your turn, then conquer the other. A hold at 7 simply wins (§473.1.a.1).
4. **The deck works without Matriarch.** Fizz, Ezreal and Merchant carry value on their own, so don't overextend just to protect her.

## The engine, and the rules it runs on

**Fill the trash.**
- **Traveling Merchant:** when it moves, discard 1, then draw 1. Moves include a Tideturner swap.
- **Ezreal, Prodigy:** when played, discard 1, then draw 2. His discount, "optional additional costs you pay cost 1 Energy or 1 Power less", covers Rampage's optional Body and Mercenary's Accelerate.
- **Shadow Order Disciple:** when it moves, you may burn 1 (the top card of your deck to the trash) for +1 Might this turn.
- **Zaun Warrens:** when you conquer there, discard 1, then draw 1.
- **Combat:** your units dying in fights.

**Use the trash.**
- **Fizz, Trickster** (3 Energy, 1 Power): when played, cast a spell of 3 Energy or less from your trash, ignoring its Energy cost but **paying its Power**, then recycle it. Every spell in the main deck qualifies. Best targets: Rampage (removal), Punch First (on an attacker before combat), Star-Crossed.
- **Tail-Cloaked Matriarch** (4 Energy): Empower costs 2 Energy and a Chaos. When she becomes Empowered, play a unit from your trash with Energy cost 3 or less and Power cost 1 or less **to your base, ignoring its cost**. Fizz and Ezreal are the best revives, because their play effects fire again.
  - **Empowered is permanent** until she leaves the board or something disempowers her (§443), so normally she revives once.
- **Tornado Warrior** (Hidden): when played from face down, you may empower something there; it's disempowered at end of turn.
  - **Hide Tornado where Matriarch is.** The flip empowers her for free, so she revives now. Because Tornado disempowers her at end of turn, she can be empowered again later, by her own Empower or another Tornado.
  - A flip is free on their turn too, so an attack on that battlefield can be met with a Tornado flip that brings back a body.
- **Star-Crossed** (Reaction): returns one of their units **and one of yours** to hand. Pick your own Fizz (another recast), Ezreal (another loot) or a used Matriarch (she can revive again when replayed).

**Hidden rules** (§811): you hide only on your turn, at a battlefield you control, one card each. **From the next turn on**, the card has Reaction and is played ignoring its whole cost, Power included. A hidden unit must go to that battlefield. Lose the battlefield and the card is trashed (§323.6.a).

## The fighters

**Staunch Survivor** (2 Energy, 1 Body): Assault 2 and Shield 2, so it attacks and defends at 4. On turn 1, a free 2-drop (Merchant, Tideturner) is better; play Staunch on turn 2 off the Gold, unless it's your only play.

**Graves, Blasting Through** (5 Energy, 1 Power): when he attacks or defends, he deals damage equal to his Might to every enemy unit there, and deals no combat damage himself.
- **Pump him before the fight.** His trigger goes on the chain when combat starts, and while it's there only Reactions can be played; Punch First is an Action. So cast it in your main phase, before he moves: +5 makes the blast 9.
- The damage is read when the trigger resolves (§359.3.f.2), so a Reaction in response still counts.

**Vex, Apathetic** (4 Energy; Deflect): when an opponent plays a unit while Vex is at a battlefield, stun it, and they can't move it this turn.
- Any battlefield counts, and a hidden card played from face down counts as played.
- It shuts down Ambush, units that enter ready, and surprise flips. Keep Vex on a battlefield.

**Rengar, Trophy Hunter** (5 Energy, 1 Power; 6 Might; Ambush): he can be played to a battlefield where you have units, with Reaction speed (§822.1.b), or, by his own text, where **they** have units.
- So he can drop into their attack as a 6-Might defender, or into their battlefield to start a fight.
- Against Mageseeker Warden he can only come down at base (rulings).

**Switcheroo** (Hidden): swap the Might of two units at the same battlefield this turn. **Hide it first when a big attacker is coming.** From face down it's free; from hand it's 2 Energy and 2 Chaos.

**Mercenary** (4 Energy, 1 Power, from the Champion Zone): once you've spent two Power this turn, she has **6 Might and Ganking**.
- **Punch First alone switches her on** (2 Power), then adds +5.
- With Accelerate she pays two Power herself and arrives ready.
- She closes best with a partner beside her: Staunch, Graves or a pump.

**Last Rites** (gear): Equip costs a Chaos plus recycling 2 cards from your trash, for +2 Might while attached (§137.3). It competes with Fizz and Matriarch for the trash, so equip it late, when the trash is full.

**Edge of Night** (Hidden gear): from face down, attach it to a unit of yours there for a surprise +2.

## Turns

- **Turn 1:** a free 2-drop (Merchant, Tideturner). Staunch only if it's your only play.
- **Turns 2–3:** take the open battlefields. Ezreal and Merchant start filling the trash, so discard with Fizz and Matriarch in mind. Hide from turn 2 where you hold.
- **Turns 3–5:** Fizz when there's a good spell in the trash. Matriarch, with a Tornado hidden beside her if you have one. Vex onto a battlefield.
- **Closing:** hold one battlefield, break the other. Mercenary (Punch First), Graves (pumped first) or a Rengar ambush.

## Matchups (reading only; untested)

- **Ambush and hidden decks** (Rengar, Kha'Zix, Evelynn): Vex on a battlefield. Keep a face-down card where they'll attack.
- **Big breakers** (Astral Heron decks: Irelia, Vex): Switcheroo face down; Star-Crossed held up (3 Energy, 1 Power); Rebuke from the sideboard.
- **Kai'Sa:** lots of removal, often aimed at Matriarch. Lean on Fizz and Ezreal, don't put everything on one battlefield, and side in **Unyielding Spirit** (prevents a turn of spell and ability damage).
- **Kennen:** Jun warns that Kennen abuses Zaun Warrens, so pick Sandswept Tomb or Sigil of the Storm as your battlefield.
- **Jayce and gear decks:** side in **An Offer You Can't Refuse** and **Acceptable Losses**.
- **Spell-heavy decks:** the second **Sabotage** (look at their hand, take the trick before the big fight).
