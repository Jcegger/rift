## The short version

*Read before every round.*

**What the deck does: it outlasts them, and turns Gold into cards.** Cheap bodies take battlefields. Merchant, Ezreal and Disciple fill the trash with **choices**: a spell for Fizz, a unit for Matriarch. **Fizz** recasts a spell from the trash; **Matriarch** plays a unit from the trash for free; **Innovator** turns spare Gold into cards. In a long game you still have bodies and tricks when they've run out.

**The legend's part: renewable Power.** Hides, Punch First, Switcheroo, Sabotage, Star-Crossed, Ezreal, Fizz and his recasts, Staunch, Matriarch's Empower, Innovator's Accelerate and draws, and Mercenary all spend Power. Kills (Rampage, won fights) ready the legend for a second Gold. **Energy is what runs out**, so spend Gold freely on the Power side.

**Your turn, in order:** lethal check, both players → count Gold and runes → **pump before you move** (Punch First on the attacker in your main phase; in a fight only Reactions can answer a trigger) → take open battlefields → develop (Fizz and Matriarch when the trash has targets) → **hide at every battlefield you hold** → fight.

**The rules that matter most:**
1. **Pay Power with Gold first; recycle a rune only for the rest.** The legend starts every turn ready, so your first recycle makes a Gold. A second recycle makes nothing, unless an enemy unit died in between: **recycle, kill, recycle.**
2. **Innovator: draw when Gold is piling up, take the Gold when you're short.** He triggers each time he exhausts, usually once a turn when he moves or attacks.
3. **Discard on purpose.** Pitch a spell of 3 Energy or less if Fizz is coming, or a unit of 3 Energy and 1 Power or less if Matriarch is.
4. **Small bodies hold and loot; they don't trade.** Fight with a pump or a face-down card ready.
5. **The eighth point by Conquer needs both battlefields scored this turn** (§473.1.b). Hold one at the start of your turn, then conquer the other. A hold at 7 simply wins (§473.1.a.1).

**Mulligan:** keep a 2-drop plus something for turn 3. Ship hands of only small bodies, or no play before turn 3.

## The engine, and the rules it runs on

**Fill the trash.**
- **Traveling Merchant:** when it moves, discard 1, then draw 1. Moves include a Tideturner swap.
- **Ezreal, Prodigy:** when played, discard 1, then draw 2. His discount ("optional additional costs you pay cost 1 Energy or 1 Power less") covers Rampage's optional Body and Mercenary's Accelerate.
- **Shadow Order Disciple:** when it moves, you may burn 1 (the top card of your deck to the trash) for +1 Might this turn.
- **Zaun Warrens:** when you conquer there, discard 1, then draw 1.

**Use the trash.**
- **Fizz, Trickster** (3 Energy, 1 Power): when played, cast a spell of 3 Energy or less from your trash, ignoring its Energy cost but **paying its Power**, then recycle it. Every spell in the main deck qualifies. Best targets: Rampage, Punch First (on an attacker before combat), and Grim Resolve, which costs no Power, so its recast is free.
- **Tail-Cloaked Matriarch** (4 Energy): Empower costs 2 Energy and a Chaos. When she becomes Empowered, play a unit from your trash with Energy cost 3 or less and Power cost 1 or less **to your base, ignoring its cost**. Best revives: Fizz, Ezreal, Innovator.
  - **Empowered is permanent** until she leaves the board or is disempowered (§443), so she normally revives once.
- **Tornado Warrior** (Hidden): when played from face down, you may empower something there; it's disempowered at end of turn. Hidden next to Matriarch, the flip makes her revive for free, and she can be empowered again later. **That combo is a bonus;** most of the time Tornado is a 3-Might body you hide for a Power and flip free.
- **Star-Crossed** (Reaction): returns one of their units **and one of yours** to hand. Pick your own Fizz, Ezreal or a used Matriarch, to replay for value.
- **Reckless Innovator** (3 Energy, Accelerate): *"When I become exhausted, choose one: play a Gold gear token exhausted; or pay a Chaos to draw 1."* Accelerate makes him enter ready, which isn't an exhaust, so it doesn't trigger him; moving or attacking does. Matriarch can revive him.

**Hidden rules** (§811): you hide only on your turn, at a battlefield you control, one card each. **From the next turn on**, the card has Reaction and is played ignoring its whole cost, Power included. A hidden unit must go to that battlefield. Lose the battlefield and the card is trashed (§323.6.a).

## The fighters and tricks

**Staunch Survivor** (2 Energy, 1 Body): Assault 2 and Shield 2, so it attacks and defends at 4. On turn 1 a free 2-drop is better; Staunch is fine on turn 1 when it's your only play (the recycled rune makes your first Gold).

**Vex, Apathetic** (4 Energy; Deflect): when an opponent plays a unit while Vex is at a battlefield, stun it, and they can't move it this turn. Any battlefield counts, and a card played from face down counts as played. It shuts down Ambush, units that enter ready, and surprise flips. **Keep Vex on a battlefield.**

**Punch First** (1 Energy, 2 Body): +5 this turn. **It alone switches Mercenary on.**

**Grim Resolve** (2 Energy; Action): +3 to a friendly unit this turn (its XP rider does nothing here). Free when Fizz recasts it.

**Edge of Night** (Hidden gear): from face down, attach it to a unit of yours there for +2 while attached. **It outlives the unit:** unattached gear at a battlefield is recalled to your base (§149.3, §323.6), ready to equip again for a Chaos.

**Switcheroo** (Hidden; 2 Energy, 2 Chaos): swap the Might of two units at the same battlefield this turn. **Hide it when a big attacker is coming;** from face down it's free.
- **If they kill or sacrifice their unit in response,** before it resolves, the swap fizzles: that unit's Might reads null (§359.3.e.12). It happened on 25 Oct against a Sacrifice on Thousand-Tailed Watcher. Once it has resolved, your unit keeps the swapped Might for the turn, because a swap is two separate +/− effects (rules FAQ).

**Mercenary** (4 Energy, 1 Power, from the Champion Zone): once you've spent two Power this turn, she has **6 Might and Ganking**. With Punch First she's 11. With Accelerate she pays two Power herself and arrives ready. She closes best with a partner beside her.

**Defy-proof:** Defy counters spells costing 4 Energy or less **and** 1 Power or less. Punch First, Switcheroo and Rebuke (sideboard) cost 2 Power, so Defy can't touch them.

## Turns

- **Turn 1:** a free 2-drop (Merchant, Tideturner, Disciple). Staunch if it's your only play.
- **Turn 2:** Staunch or another 2-drop. Hide where you hold.
- **Turn 3:** Innovator (Accelerate if you can pay it) or Ezreal.
- **Turns 4–5:** Vex onto a battlefield. Matriarch when the trash has a target. Fizz when there's a good spell in the trash.
- **Every turn:** hide at each battlefield you hold, and spend spare Gold (Innovator's draws, Punch First, recasts).
- **Closing:** hold one battlefield, break the other with Mercenary (Punch First) or a pumped unit.

## Battlefields

- **Game 1: Zaun Warrens** (a loot on conquer, which fills the trash). Against Kennen, Sandswept Tomb instead (Jun: Kennen abuses Zaun Warrens).
- **Going second: Sigil of the Storm.** The conqueror must recycle a rune; going second you have the extra rune (§487.7).
- **Going first: Sandswept Tomb.** Spells choosing your unit there cost 1 Power less.
- A battlefield used in a decided game is retired for the match (§488.5). **Avoid fighting on their power battlefield** (Void Gate adds +1 to damage aimed at units there).

## What to log (the journal's questions)

1. **Innovator:** did his draws keep your hand full? Is Gold still piling up?
2. **Fizz:** did he have a spell to recast?
3. **Turn 1:** did you have a free play?
4. **Small bodies:** did they get picked off by pings or out-sized?
5. **Losses:** what beat you? Use [strategy.md](../docs/strategy.md)'s seven mistake types.

**First swaps to try if the logs ask:**

| big attackers breaking your holds | Siren's Song (sideboard) for Gust |
