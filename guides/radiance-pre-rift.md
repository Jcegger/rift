## 2. Building in 45 minutes

Sealed is not constructed. You can't build the deck you want; you **build the best deck your
pool allows**, and the pool is mostly commons. The order that works, borrowed from decades of
Magic sealed and matched to Riftbound's rules:

1. **Open and sort by domain** (6 piles + colorless). Put the champion pack's cards apart.
2. **Pull out the removal first**, every pile. Removal is the scarcest and most valuable thing
   in sealed. Then the **bombs**, cards that win the game if unanswered.
3. **Read the champion pack.** It's the most coherent part of your pool: a legend plus cards
   built for it. **Start from its two domains** unless they're thin in your boosters.
4. **Pick the third domain** for what the first two lack, usually **removal**, not more
   threats. A third domain is one of sealed's big advantages (Riot's guide says the same).
5. **Count playables** in your two or three domains. You want **~15 units** that can fight,
   **~5 of them costing 2**, plus removal, a few tricks and card flow (Riot's numbers).
6. **Cut to exactly 25.** More cards only dilute your best ones. Running out of cards isn't a
   loss in Riftbound (it gives your opponent a point), and a 25-card deck won't run out in a
   normal game.
7. **Runes: count Power pips** in each domain and split the 12 to match, e.g. **5/5/2** with a
   small third domain, **6/6** with two. A third domain you only need Energy from can be 1–2
   runes.
8. **Battlefields:** pick ones that help you, or use a blank rather than one that helps them
   more.
9. **No-legend check:** if your legend's ability does little with the cards you have, go
   without it and take the **extra card**. That's not a fallback, it's a real choice.

**Traps:** building a two-card combo (you won't draw it), playing 30 cards "for options", and
falling in love with a rare that doesn't fit.

## 3. What wins sealed

These are the parts of Magic's sealed wisdom that carry straight over:

- **Removal and bombs decide most games.** A pool's three best removal spells usually matter
  more than its next ten units.
- **Fights are fairer and closer.** There's less removal and movement than in constructed,
  so **winning showdowns matters more** (riftbound.gg's Pre-Rift guide). Combat tricks are
  worth a slot or two, especially cheap ones.
- **The curve is everything.** A hand with nothing to do on turn 1–2 loses the battlefield
  race. That's why **~5 two-drops**.
- **Go first if you can.** riftbound.gg's guide argues going second is worse in sealed than
  in constructed.
- **Your habits still apply.** Spend Power selectively and take rigged fights. Sealed decks
  are slower and clunkier, which rewards patience more, not less.
- **It's a learning event.** Every Radiance keyword you meet at a Pre-Rift is one you won't
  misplay at the 9 November Skirmish.

rare-or-better slots). Ratings are a read of the cards. **★ = take it into almost any deck in
its domain.** Unrevealed cards (eight slots as of 13 October) aren't listed.

**2-drops** to reach five: Entrenched Boar (Calm), Siren of the Shallows (Chaos, Ganking),
Strigian Oracle (Mind), Lingering Animus (Fury), Overloaded Traveler and Sneaky Zeebles
(Body), Meticulous Restorationist (Order), Amateur Demolitionist (Fury).

### Card flow

**The Good Stuff** (Chaos U, Deploy: discard 1, draw 3), **Life from Stone** (Calm C: draw 1
and a rune), **Blossoming Dreamroot** (Calm C, Deploy: draw when you win a fight there),
**Fanfare** (Mind C: draw on your next three unit plays; only in a unit-heavy turn).

### Traps

- **Avalanche** (Body U) hits *every* unit in *every* base, yours too.
- **Suave Negotiator** (Chaos C) draws your opponent a card as well.
- **Command: Attack**, **Improvised Projectile** and **Nazuman Ntofo** need gear you may not
  have.
- **Party Crasher**, **Piltovan Guidebot**, **Siren's Song**: narrow.
- **Momentary Companion** dies at your next turn if you flip it on theirs.
- **Disposal Expert** only enters ready with no other units.
- **Cosmic Vista** (battlefield) needs an empty Rune Deck.

## 5. Your champion pack, legend by legend

The pack is the most coherent thing you'll open. Here's what each legend wants in sealed, and
how much it needs from the boosters. Ratings are a read of the cards.

| legend | domains | in sealed | build toward |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Riven, The Exile** | Calm/Chaos | **strong, and generic.** Units you play to a battlefield you control enter ready. It works with any units | lots of units; anything that exhausts a unit for value; Fury or Mind removal as the third domain |
| **Ziggs, Hexplosives Expert** | Fury/Chaos | **strong.** Pay 1 Energy + 1 Power to make a Bomb: repeatable removal, and sealed is short on removal | Bombs (Amateur Demolitionist, Satchel Charge), Fury removal |
| **K'Sante, Pride of Nazumah** | Calm/Body | **good** if you have Shield units: Assault equal to Shield | Stoic Ferryman, Consult the Heavens, Wrath of the Freljord, Hold 'Em |
| **Mordekaiser, Iron Revenant** | Fury/Order | **good** late: Power from units and gear in your trash | big Order and Fury units; Fury removal |
| **Ekko, Boy Who Shattered Time** | Fury/Mind | **fine.** Your next unit is 2 Energy + 2 Power cheaper but dies at end of turn: a big attacker for one turn | 6–9 cost units; Chronobreak (his signature) keeps one |
| **Jarvan IV, Exemplar of Demacia** | Body/Order | **fine** with Deploy gear: move a unit there | Pillaged Armory, Concert Poster, Demacian Standard |
| **Seraphine, Starry-Eyed Songstress** | Mind/Order | **fair:** exhaust her and three units to make a spell 2 cheaper | tokens (Mechsmith, Concert Poster, Rousing Display) |
| **Evelynn, Agony's Embrace** | Body/Chaos | **fair:** draws only when you play from facedown, and boosters hold few Hidden cards | Hidden cards; Body tricks |
| **Orianna, Lady of Clockwork** | Calm/Mind | **weakest here:** turns a gear into a unit, and sealed pools have little gear | gear; otherwise consider no legend |

**If your legend's colors are thin in your boosters, drop the legend.** Pick your best three
domains and take the extra card. A Champion Unit from any of them can still be your Chosen
Champion.

**If you open Riven:** it's practice for 23 October. The exhaust engine (Meditation-style
cards, Reckless Innovator, the Riven units) mostly won't be in a sealed pool, but the core
idea is the same: **units arrive ready at the battlefield you hold.**

## 5a. What's in each champion pack

Each pack is **15 cards**: the Legend, its rare Champion Unit, a Battlefield and **12 support
cards, one copy each**. Everything else comes from your five boosters. Copies stack: a booster
copy of a pack card is a second copy in your deck (no copy limit in sealed).

**Riot hasn't published these lists.** They come from two fan sites, riftbound.org and
riftbound.zone (checked 20 Oct 2026), which agree card for card on all nine. Every card is real
and in your pack's two domains, checked against the card catalog. If your pack differs, **trust the pack**.
Each card reads *cost · name — type, domain. Text.* Cost is Energy, plus Power as P. Text is shortened (keyword reminders cut); read the card for exact wording.

## 6. Radiance rules worth knowing before you play

- **Disarm** (new): the first time a unit with Disarm attacks each combat, an enemy unit there
  gets −1 Might (−2 for Disarm 2). Several Disarm instances add into one trigger.
- **Deploy** (new): gear you play onto a battlefield, even an enemy one. **It dies when an
  opponent holds that battlefield.**
- **Show Off** (new): reveal a matching card from your hand, or pick a friendly one on the
  board. Facedown cards can't be shown off.
- **K'Sante's legend** adds Assault equal to Shield: a unit with Shield 2 and Assault 2 gets
  Assault 4 (Riot's FAQ).
- **Encore** (Seraphine's signature): you must choose two *different* modes.
- **Equipment that becomes a unit loses its Equipment text** (Riot's FAQ): Blade of the Exile
  as a unit is a 3-Might unit without Ganking. This matters if you open Orianna.
- **Hidden:** a hidden unit flipped from facedown must be played at its own battlefield, and
  its play effects must choose targets there (§811.1.d).
- **No legend or no champion:** you draw your extra card at the start of your first Beginning
  Phase, not in your opening hand.
